## E-6.1 Across the globe

*选必三 Unit 1 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频中 ｜ 话题：全球视野与学校 ｜ 语法：将来时的不同形式 ｜ 本单元 62 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："The train leaves at 7." 用一般现在时表示将来，为什么？

### 30 秒概述

本单元从"让学校更有意义"出发（Making school meaningful），学**将来时的不同形式**，写一段**地点描写**。词汇集中在偏远地区、教育、自然与探索。

### 30 秒自评

1. 一般现在时可以表示按时刻表发生的将来动作吗？
2. "be about to do" 表示"即将做"吗？
3. "be to do" 常表示计划、命令或注定吗？

> **答案**：1. 可以（The train leaves at 7.）　2. 是　3. 是

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **remote** adj. 偏远的：a **remote** village 偏远村庄；**remotely** adv. 遥远地
- **minority** n. 少数；少数民族：a **minority** of 少数；**minority** group 少数群体
- **rigid** adj. 僵化的；坚硬的：a **rigid** system 僵化的制度；**rigidity** n. 僵化
- **curriculum** n. 课程：the school **curriculum** 学校课程；**extracurricular** adj. 课外的
- **enormous** adj. 巨大的：an **enormous** difference 巨大差异；**enormously** adv. 非常
- **fluency** n. 流利：**fluency** in English 英语流利；**fluent** adj. 流利的
- **wilderness** n. 荒野：the **wilderness** 荒野；**wild** adj. 野生的
- **horizon** n. 地平线；眼界：on the **horizon** 在地平线上；broaden one's **horizons** 开阔眼界
- **impressive** adj. 令人印象深刻的：an **impressive** result 令人印象深刻的结果；**impress** v. 使印象深刻
- **resolve** v./n. 解决；决心：**resolve** a problem 解决问题；**resolution** n. 决心

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| broaden one's horizons | 开阔眼界 | Travel helps **broaden our horizons**. |
| be isolated from | 与……隔绝 | The village **is isolated from** the city. |
| rely on | 依靠 | The school **relies on** volunteers. |
| make a difference to | 对……有影响 | The project **made a difference to** local children. |
| in demand | 有需求 | English teachers are **in demand**. |
| overcome difficulties | 克服困难 | They **overcame** many **difficulties**. |
| be relevant to | 与……相关 | The course **is relevant to** real life. |
| take shape | 成形 | A new plan began to **take shape**. |

### 📐 语法：将来时的不同形式

| 形式 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| will do | 临时决定/预测 | I **will call** you later. |
| be going to do | 计划/有迹象 | We **are going to visit** the village. |
| 现在进行时 | 已安排的将来 | We **are leaving** tomorrow. |
| 一般现在时 | 时刻表/日程 | The train **leaves** at 7. |
| be about to do | 即将 | The class **is about to start**. |
| be to do | 计划/命令/注定 | You **are to report** at nine. |

**要点**
1. 时刻表用一般现在时；个人安排用现在进行时
2. be going to 强调"打算"或"有迹象"；will 强调"临时/意愿"
3. be to do 较正式，常见于通知、规定

**易错边界**
1. 时刻表用 will：The train will leave at 7 every day.（×，习惯用 leaves）
2. be about to 加具体时间：is about to start at 9（×，about to 不接具体时间）
3. will 与 be going to 混用导致"计划性"不清

**三个练习**
1. The film ________ (start) at 8 tonight. → **starts**
2. Look at those clouds! It ________ (rain). → **is going to rain**
3. We ________ (meet) at the gate at six. → **are meeting**

### ✍️ 写作：地点描写

**结构**：位置与整体印象 → 2–3 个细节（自然/人文）→ 人的活动 → 感受

**五个可套用句式**
1. **Located in..., ... is a place where...** 位于……，……是一个……的地方。
2. **What strikes you first is...** 最先打动你的是……
3. **Beyond..., you can see...** 在……之外，你能看到……
4. **The people here...** 这里的人……
5. **It is a place that...** 这是一个……的地方。

### 📖 阅读技能：识别"问题—方案"文

教育类文章常提"资源不均—远程教育—效果"。读时标出问题、措施与结果；数字（学生人数、距离）常是论据。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：remote / curriculum / enormous / fluency / resolve

1. The school is in a ______ area.
2. The new ______ includes more art lessons.
3. There is an ______ gap between the two schools.
4. Her ______ in English surprised us.
5. They worked together to ______ the problem.

**解析**：1. remote　2. curriculum　3. enormous　4. fluency　5. resolve

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题 curriculum 复数为 curricula，注意语境单数。

**变式**：用 broaden one's horizons 造句，表达"旅行能开阔眼界"。→ Travel can broaden our horizons.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用适当的将来时形式填空：
1. The concert ________ (begin) at 7:30 according to the poster.
2. I ________ (visit) my grandma this weekend; we've planned it.
3. Watch out! You ________ (fall).
4. You ________ (not be) late again, or you'll miss the bus.

**解析**：1. begins　2. am going to visit（或 am visiting）　3. are going to fall　4. are not to be

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题用 will begin（时刻表用一般现在时）；第 3 题用 will fall（有迹象用 be going to）。

**变式**：把 "We are about to leave." 改为 "We are to leave." 并说明语气差别。→ are to leave 更正式，含安排/规定意味。

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 这所偏远学校的课程很有限，但学生们对知识有着巨大的渴望。
2. 明天早上七点我们将在校门口集合，然后出发去那个村庄。
3. 这个项目旨在开阔学生的眼界，并帮助他们克服困难。

**参考译文**：
1. The **curriculum** in this **remote** school is limited, but the students have an **enormous** desire for knowledge.
2. We **are meeting** at the school gate at seven tomorrow morning and then **setting off** for the village.
3. The project **aims to broaden** students' **horizons** and help them **overcome difficulties**.

**采分点**：① 一般现在时/形容词搭配；② 现在进行时表将来安排；③ aim to do + broaden horizons。

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 句用 will meet（已安排用现在进行时更自然）；第 3 句 aim 后漏 to。

**变式**：把第 2 句改为 "be going to" 结构：We are going to meet at the school gate at seven tomorrow morning.

> 答案：① 一般现在时　② 不能　③ 已安排好的近期计划

## E-6.2 Things that matter

*选必三 Unit 2 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：物品与价值 ｜ 语法：主语从句 ｜ 本单元 62 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："What he said were true." 这句话对吗？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讲"我们生活里的东西"：读一篇关于物品与价值的文章（The stuff in our lives），学**主语从句**，为**讲故事比赛**写一个故事。词汇集中在拥有、价值、判断与比较。

### 30 秒自评

1. 主语从句的谓语用单数还是复数？
2. "It is important that..." 中 it 是什么成分？
3. whether 可以引导主语从句吗？

> **答案**：1. 单数（What he said **is** true.）　2. 形式主语　3. 可以

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **possession** n. 拥有；财物：be in **possession** of 拥有；**possessions** 财物
- **permanent** adj. 永久的：a **permanent** job 固定工作；**permanently** adv. 永久地
- **temporary** adj. 暂时的：a **temporary** solution 临时方案；**temporarily** adv. 暂时
- **resist** v. 抵制；抵抗：**resist** the temptation 抵制诱惑；**resistance** n. 抵抗
- **tendency** n. 趋势；倾向：a **tendency** to do 倾向于做；**tend** v. 倾向于
- **reputation** n. 名声：have a **reputation** for 以……闻名；**reputable** adj. 声誉好的
- **precious** adj. 珍贵的：**precious** memories 珍贵回忆；**precious** metals 贵金属
- **comparison** n. 比较：in **comparison** with 与……相比；**compare** v. 比较
- **secure** adj./v. 安全的；获得：a **secure** job 稳定的工作；**security** n. 安全
- **justify** v. 证明……正当：**justify** doing 为做……辩护；**justification** n. 理由

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be in possession of | 拥有 | He **is in possession of** a rare book. |
| resist the temptation | 抵制诱惑 | She **resisted the temptation** to buy it. |
| have a reputation for | 以……闻名 | The shop **has a reputation for** quality. |
| in comparison with | 与……相比 | **In comparison with** last year, sales rose. |
| be worth the money | 值这个价 | The old vase **is worth the money**. |
| secure a place | 获得名额 | She **secured a place** at university. |
| justify doing | 为做……辩护 | Nothing can **justify cheating**. |
| let go of | 放下；舍弃 | He learned to **let go of** old things. |

### 📐 语法：主语从句

| 引导词 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| that | **That he passed** the exam surprised us. |
| whether | **Whether we go** depends on the weather. |
| what | **What matters** is honesty. |
| who / why / how | **Who will lead** the team is unknown. |
| it 形式主语 | **It is** clear **that** he is right. |

**要点**
1. 主语从句作主语，谓语用**单数**
2. that 引导主语从句时不能省略（句首）；用 it 形式主语时可省
3. what = the thing(s) that，不能与 that 混用

**易错边界**
1. 谓语用复数：What he said **were** true.（×）
2. 句首 that 省略：**He is honest** is known to all.（×，应为 That he is honest...）
3. what 与 that 混用：**That** you need is time.（×，应为 What）

**三个练习**
1. ________ he will come is still unknown. → **Whether**
2. ________ surprised me was his courage. → **What**
3. 改错：It is said that he is honest, that is true. → **...and that is true.**

### ✍️ 写作：讲故事（storytelling）

**结构**：背景（1 段）→ 冲突/转折（1–2 段）→ 结局与寓意（1 段）

**五个可套用句式**
1. **It all began on a rainy afternoon.** 一切都始于一个雨天的下午。
2. **Just as he was about to..., something happened.** 就在他正要……时，事情发生了。
3. **What he found there changed everything.** 他在那里发现的东西改变了一切。
4. **In the end, he realized that...** 最后，他意识到……
5. **The story teaches us that...** 这个故事告诉我们……

### 📖 阅读技能：区分"物品"与"意义"

文章常先写物品（old vase, ticket, letter），再写它承载的记忆。注意 **worth, value, mean, remind** 等词——它们把"物"与"意义"连接起来，是主旨题的落点。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：possession / permanent / resist / reputation / precious

1. The museum is famous for its ______ collection.
2. He could not ______ the temptation to open the box.
3. The shop has a good ______ for honest service.
4. A ______ job gives him a sense of security.
5. Health is our most ______ possession.

**解析**：1. precious　2. resist　3. reputation　4. permanent　5. precious

**💥 陷阱**：第 5 题 possession 与 precious 搭配，注意词性。

**变式**：用 in comparison with 造句，表达"与去年相比，今年的销量更高"。→ In comparison with last year, sales are higher this year.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用适当的引导词填空：
1. ________ we should keep the old house is a difficult question.
2. ________ he did was not what we expected.
3. It is clear ________ she is right.

**解析**：1. Whether　2. What　3. that

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题用 That（×，缺主语）；第 3 题 that 可省略但书面语常保留。

**变式**：把 "That he lied made us angry." 改为 it 形式主语。→ It made us angry that he lied.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 真正重要的不是我们拥有多少东西，而是我们如何看待它们。
2. 与中国其他城市相比，这座小城以保存完好的老建筑闻名。
3. 他最终意识到，有些东西的价值无法用金钱衡量。

**参考译文**：
1. **What really matters** is not how much we **possess** but how we see what we have.
2. **In comparison with** other cities in China, this small town **has a reputation for** its well-preserved old buildings.
3. He finally realized **that** some things are **precious** beyond money.

**采分点**：① what 引导主语从句 + not...but；② in comparison with + reputation for；③ realize that + beyond。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句用 That 引导主语从句（×）；第 2 句 comparison 后介词用 to（应为 with）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为强调句：It is not how much we possess but how we see things that really matters.

> 答案：① 单数　② what 在从句中作成分，that 不作成分　③ 与……相比
