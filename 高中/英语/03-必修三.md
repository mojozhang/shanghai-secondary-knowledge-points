## E-3.1 The media

*必修三 Unit 1 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：媒体与新闻 ｜ 语法：-ing 与 -ed 形式作宾语补足语 ｜ 本单元 55 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："I saw the journalist interviewed the star." 这句话对吗？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"媒体"：读一篇关于记者工作的文章（Journalists on the job），学 **-ing 与 -ed 形式作宾语补足语**，写一篇**影评（film review）**。词汇集中在新闻、报道、核实与娱乐。

### 30 秒自评

1. "see somebody do" 与 "see somebody doing" 有什么区别？
2. "have something done" 表示"让某事被做"吗？
3. 感官动词后的宾语补足语可以用 -ed 形式吗？

> **答案**：1. do 表示看到全过程，doing 表示看到正在进行　2. 是（也可表示"遭遇"）　3. 可以（I heard the song sung in English.）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **journalist** n. 记者：a **journalist** for 为……工作的记者；**journalism** n. 新闻业
- **authority** n. 权威；当局：the **authorities** 当局；an **authority** on 在……方面的权威
- **annual** adj. 每年的：an **annual** report 年度报告；**annually** adv. 每年
- **spot** v./n. 发现；地点：**spot** a mistake 发现错误；a tourist **spot** 旅游景点
- **ensure** v. 确保：**ensure** that 确保；**ensure** safety 确保安全
- **affect** v. 影响：**affect** the result 影响结果；be **affected** by 受……影响
- **confirm** v. 确认：**confirm** the news 证实消息；**confirmation** n. 确认
- **publish** v. 出版；发表：**publish** a book 出版书；**publication** n. 出版物
- **plot** n. 情节：the **plot** of a film 电影情节；a simple **plot** 简单的情节
- **entertaining** adj. 有趣的：an **entertaining** film 娱乐性强的电影；**entertainment** n. 娱乐

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be responsible for | 对……负责 | A journalist **is responsible for** the truth. |
| check the facts | 核实事实 | Always **check the facts** before publishing. |
| affect the audience | 影响观众 | The report **affected** a wide **audience**. |
| confirm the news | 证实消息 | The office **confirmed the news** later. |
| publish an article | 发表文章 | She **published an article** about the event. |
| be based on | 基于 | The film **is based on** a true story. |
| attract attention | 吸引注意 | The headline **attracted** much **attention**. |
| in the public eye | 在公众视野中 | Celebrities live **in the public eye**. |

### 📐 语法：-ing 与 -ed 形式作宾语补足语

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| see/hear/watch + sb + doing | 看到/听到某人正在做 | I saw him **writing** the report. |
| see/hear/watch + sb + do | 看到/听到某人做（全过程） | I saw him **write** the report. |
| have/get + sth + done | 让某事被做；遭遇 | I had my camera **repaired**. |
| keep/leave + sb/sth + doing | 使……持续 | The news kept people **talking**. |
| find + sb/sth + done | 发现……被…… | She found the door **locked**. |

**要点**
1. 感官动词 + doing（进行）与 + do（全过程）意义不同
2. have sth done 的主语不一定执行动作
3. -ed 作补语表示被动或完成

**易错边界**
1. 感官动词后误加 to：I saw him to write.（×）
2. have sth done 与 have sb do 混用：I had him repair it.（√，让他修）/ I had it repaired.（√，让别人修）
3. -ed 与 -ing 补语混淆：I found the film boring.（√，电影令人无聊）/ I found the film bored.（×）

**三个练习**
1. I heard her ________ (sing) in the next room. → **singing**
2. He had his hair ________ (cut) yesterday. → **cut**
3. 改错：We watched the match played in the rain. → **playing**（比赛正在进行）

### ✍️ 写作：影评（film review）

**结构**：片名与类型 → 一句话剧情（不剧透）→ 亮点（表演/音乐/画面）→ 不足（可选）→ 推荐指数

**五个可套用句式**
1. **... is a film that...** ……是一部……的电影。
2. **The story is based on...** 故事改编自……
3. **What impressed me most was...** 最打动我的是……
4. **The film is worth watching because...** 这部电影值得看，因为……
5. **I would recommend it to anyone who...** 我把它推荐给所有……的人。

### 📖 阅读技能：新闻标题与导语

新闻标题常省略冠词和 be 动词（**Journalist wins award**），导语（第一段）回答 who/what/when/where/why。读标题先猜主题，再读导语核对；细节题按关键词定位。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：journalist / authority / confirm / publish / plot

1. The ______ interviewed three witnesses.
2. The police ______ the identity of the driver.
3. The novel was ______ last year.
4. The ______ of the film is easy to follow.
5. She is an ______ on ancient Chinese poetry.

**解析**：1. journalist　2. confirmed　3. published　4. plot　5. authority

**💥 陷阱**：第 5 题 authority 表示"权威人士"，前用 an。

**变式**：用 be based on 造句，表达"这部电影改编自真实事件"。→ The film is based on a true story.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用括号中词的正确形式填空：
1. I watched the children ________ (play) football.
2. She had her computer ________ (fix) yesterday.
3. We found the door ________ (lock).
4. The teacher kept us ________ (read) for an hour.

**解析**：1. playing　2. fixed　3. locked　4. reading

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题 find + 宾语 + done，表示"发现门被锁着"。

**变式**：用 "have sth done" 改写："Someone repaired my bike." → I had my bike repaired.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 记者必须核实事实，确保报道不影响无辜的人。
2. 我看到她正在编辑那篇关于电影情节的文章。
3. 这部纪录片基于真实事件，虽然有些地方令人不安，但非常值得一看。

**参考译文**：
1. A **journalist** must **check the facts** to **ensure** that the report does not **affect** innocent people.
2. I saw her **editing** the article about the film's **plot**.
3. The documentary **is based on** true events. Although some parts are **disturbing**, it is well **worth watching**.

**采分点**：① check the facts + ensure that；② see sb doing；③ be based on + although + be worth doing。

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 句用 see her edit（全过程）而非 editing（正在进行）；第 3 句 worth 后接 to watch（×）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为被动语态：The facts must be checked by a journalist to ensure that...

> 答案：① 全过程／正在进行　② 让某事被做或遭遇　③ 不能

## E-3.2 The things around us

*必修三 Unit 2 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：物品与全球链条 ｜ 语法：被动语态的各种形式 ｜ 本单元 51 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："This kind of cloth is washed well." 和 "This kind of cloth washes well." 哪句更自然？

### 30 秒概述

本单元从一件 T 恤出发，读它的"全球旅程"（The story of a T-shirt），学**被动语态的各种形式**，写一篇**描述过程**的文章。词汇集中在生产、贸易、收入与环境。

### 30 秒自评

1. 被动语态可以用于现在完成时和情态动词吗？
2. "The shirt washes well." 是主动形式表被动意义吗？
3. "need doing" 等于 "need to be done" 吗？

> **答案**：1. 可以（has been done / can be done）　2. 是　3. 是

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **cotton** n. 棉花：a **cotton** shirt 棉衬衫；**cotton** fields 棉田
- **export** v./n. 出口：**export** to 出口到；**exports** 出口商品
- **profit** n./v. 利润；获益：make a **profit** 获利；**profitable** adj. 有利可图的
- **involve** v. 涉及；使参与：be **involved** in 参与；**involve** doing 需要做
- **income** n. 收入：a steady **income** 稳定收入；**income** tax 所得税
- **quantity** n. 数量：a large **quantity** of 大量的（可数/不可数）；in **quantity** 大量地
- **majority** n. 大多数：the **majority** of 大多数；a **majority** vote 多数票
- **equipment** n. 设备（不可数）：a piece of **equipment** 一件设备；modern **equipment** 现代设备
- **advanced** adj. 先进的：**advanced** technology 先进技术；**advance** v./n. 前进
- **remarkable** adj. 显著的：a **remarkable** change 显著变化；**remarkably** adv. 非常

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be made of / from | 由……制成 | The shirt **is made of** cotton. |
| be exported to | 出口到 | Most of the cotton **is exported to** Asia. |
| be involved in | 参与；涉及 | Many workers **are involved in** the process. |
| a large quantity of | 大量的 | **A large quantity of** water **is** used. |
| the majority of | 大多数 | **The majority of** customers are young. |
| be equipped with | 配备 | The factory **is equipped with** new machines. |
| make a profit | 获利 | The company **made a profit** last year. |
| be replaced by | 被……取代 | Workers may **be replaced by** robots. |

### 📐 语法：被动语态的各种形式

| 时态/形式 | 结构 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般现在 | am/is/are done | The shirt **is made** in China. |
| 一般过去 | was/were done | It **was sold** last week. |
| 一般将来 | will be done | It **will be shipped** tomorrow. |
| 现在完成 | has/have been done | The order **has been sent**. |
| 情态动词 | can/must be done | It **can be recycled**. |
| 现在进行 | is/are being done | The road **is being repaired**. |

**要点**
1. 被动语态 = be + 过去分词；时态体现在 be 上
2. 主动形式表被动：sell / wash / write / read 等表属性（The pen **writes** smoothly.）
3. need / want / require + doing = to be done（The shirt **needs washing**.）
4. 不及物动词无被动（happen, appear, belong to）

**易错边界**
1. 漏 be：The shirt made in China.（×）
2. 用错时态：The order has sent.（×，应为 has been sent）
3. 把 happen 用于被动：The accident was happened.（×）

**三个练习**
1. The new bridge ________ (build) next year. → **will be built**
2. The letter ________ (send) already. → **has been sent**
3. 改错：The book is worth to read. → **The book is worth reading.**（或 The book is worth being read. 不自然）

### ✍️ 写作：描述过程（process）

**结构**：总起（要描述什么过程）→ 分步（first, next, then, after that, finally）→ 小结（结果/意义），多用被动语态

**五个可套用句式**
1. **The process can be divided into... steps.** 这个过程可分为……步。
2. **First, ... is collected / picked.** 首先，……被收集/采摘。
3. **Next, it is sent to... where it is...** 接着，它被送到……在那里被……
4. **After that, ...** 在那之后，……
5. **Finally, the product is ready for...** 最后，产品可以用于……

### 📖 阅读技能：读流程图与数据

流程题先找**起点**与**终点**，再看箭头之间的动词（多为被动）。数据题注意单位、年份与比较对象；"twice as many as" 这类倍数表达要还原成数字再判断。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：cotton / export / profit / involve / equipment

1. The T-shirt is made of ______.
2. The company ______ its products to over 30 countries.
3. The factory uses modern ______.
4. Many workers are ______ in the process.
5. They made a large ______ last year.

**解析**：1. cotton　2. exports　3. equipment　4. involved　5. profit

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题 equipment 不可数，不能加 s。

**变式**：用 be made from 造句，表达"纸是由木材制成的"。→ Paper is made from wood.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

把下列句子改为被动语态：
1. They grow cotton in Xinjiang.
2. Someone has sent the order.
3. We must protect the environment.

**解析**：
1. Cotton **is grown** in Xinjiang.
2. The order **has been sent**.
3. The environment **must be protected**.

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题漏 been；第 3 题 must 后漏 be。

**变式**：把 "The shirt needs washing." 改为同义句。→ The shirt needs to be washed.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 这件 T 恤由棉花制成，在另一个国家被染色，然后出口到世界各地。
2. 大多数工人参与了生产过程，但他们的收入并不高。
3. 由于采用了先进设备，这家工厂的产量显著提高。

**参考译文**：
1. The T-shirt **is made of** cotton, **is dyed** in another country, and then **is exported** all over the world.
2. **The majority of** the workers **are involved in** the production process, but their **income** is not high.
3. Because **advanced equipment** **has been adopted**, the factory's **output** has increased **remarkably**.

**采分点**：① 三个并列被动；② the majority of + be involved in；③ because 从句 + 现在完成被动。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句时态不一致（is made / was dyed）；第 3 句 output 拼写与 remarkable 副词形式。

**变式**：把第 3 句改为 "with 复合结构"：With advanced equipment adopted, the factory's output has increased remarkably.

> 答案：① has/have been done　② sell, wash, write, read 等　③ need to be done

## E-3.3 The way we are

*必修三 Unit 3 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频中 ｜ 话题：外貌与自我 ｜ 语法：动词不定式作定语 ｜ 本单元 51 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："I have no time to watch TV." 中的 to watch TV 修饰什么？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"我们如何看待自己"：读一篇关于"理想美"的文章（Ideal beauty），学**动词不定式作定语**，写一封**给建议专栏的回复**。词汇集中在外貌、代表性、性别与个人形象。

### 30 秒自评

1. 不定式作定语通常放在被修饰名词之后吗？
2. "something to eat" 中的 to eat 与 something 是动宾关系吗？
3. "the first to arrive" 中的 to arrive 修饰 the first 吗？

> **答案**：1. 是（后置）　2. 是　3. 是

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **ideal** adj./n. 理想的；理想：an **ideal** body 理想身材；**ideal** conditions 理想条件
- **representation** n. 代表；表现：a **representation** of beauty 美的呈现；**represent** v. 代表
- **generation** n. 一代人：the younger **generation** 年轻一代；**generational** adj. 代际的
- **elegant** adj. 优雅的：an **elegant** dress 优雅的裙子；**elegance** n. 优雅
- **display** v./n. 展示：on **display** 展出；**display** confidence 表现出自信
- **vary** v. 变化；不同：**vary** from...to... 从……到……不等；**variety** n. 多样性
- **context** n. 背景；语境：in the **context** of 在……背景下；**contextual** adj. 上下文的
- **gender** n. 性别：**gender** roles 性别角色；**gender** equality 性别平等
- **personality** n. 个性：a strong **personality** 鲜明的个性；**personal** adj. 个人的
- **impression** n. 印象：make an **impression** on 给……留下印象；**impress** v. 使印象深刻

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be obsessed with | 痴迷于 | Many teenagers **are obsessed with** looks. |
| make an impression on | 给……留下印象 | Her confidence **made an impression on** us. |
| vary from...to... | 从……到……不等 | Beauty standards **vary from** culture **to** culture. |
| in the context of | 在……背景下 | **In the context of** social media, images matter. |
| on display | 展出 | The photos are **on display** in the hall. |
| be judged by | 根据……评判 | People should not **be judged by** their looks. |
| a sense of identity | 认同感 | Clothes can give us **a sense of identity**. |
| break the stereotype | 打破刻板印象 | The show tries to **break the stereotype**. |

### 📐 语法：动词不定式作定语

| 结构 | 例子 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 名词 + to do | the ability **to adapt** | 表示将要/需要做的动作 |
| something/nothing + to do | something **to eat** | 动宾关系 |
| the first/last/only + to do | the first **to arrive** | 序数词后 |
| have + 名词 + to do | have no time **to waste** | 有时含被动含义 |

**要点**
1. 不定式作定语放在名词后
2. 不及物动词作定语时需加介词：a chair **to sit on**；a pen **to write with**
3. 不定式与所修饰名词有动宾关系时，注意逻辑主语

**易错边界**
1. 漏介词：a pen to write（×，应为 to write with）
2. 位置错误：the to read book（×）
3. 不定式与分词混用：the boiling water（正在烧开）/ the water to boil（要烧开的水）

**三个练习**
1. I need a chair ________ (sit). → **to sit on**
2. She is always the first ________ (come) and the last ________ (leave). → **to come；to leave**
3. 改错：There is nothing to worry. → **There is nothing to worry about.**

### ✍️ 写作：建议专栏回复（advice column reply）

**结构**：共情（1 句）→ 分析问题（2 句）→ 2–3 条具体建议 → 鼓励（1 句）

**五个可套用句式**
1. **I'm sorry to hear that you...** 听说你……我很难过。
2. **It is normal to feel...** 感到……是正常的。
3. **Here are a few things you could try.** 你可以试试这几件事。
4. **Instead of comparing yourself with others, ...** 与其和别人比较，不如……
5. **Remember that...** 记住……

### 📖 阅读技能：区分事实与观点

事实可验证（The survey covered 2,000 teenagers.），观点含评价（Beauty standards are too narrow.）。注意 **should, ought to, beautiful, ugly, better** 等词；题目问 "What is the author's opinion?" 时，找评价性表达。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：ideal / generation / display / vary / personality

1. Beauty standards ______ from culture to culture.
2. The younger ______ cares more about self-expression.
3. Her ______ makes her popular among classmates.
4. The photos are on ______ in the school hall.
5. No one has an ______ face.

**解析**：1. vary　2. generation　3. personality　4. display　5. ideal

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题 vary 的主语是复数 standards，用原形。

**变式**：用 make an impression on 造句，表达"她的演讲给观众留下深刻印象"。→ Her speech made a deep impression on the audience.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用不定式完成句子：
1. She has a strong desire ________ (succeed).
2. There is nothing ________ (worry) about.
3. He is the only person ________ (know) the truth.

**解析**：1. **to succeed**　2. **to worry**　3. **to know**

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题 worry 后保留 about（不定式作定语时介词不能丢）。

**变式**：用 "a room to live in" 造句：I finally found a room to live in.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 不同文化对美的理解不同，年轻人尤其容易受影响。
2. 与其追求理想的外表，不如培养能让你自信的能力。
3. 给建议专栏写信的那个女孩说，她再也不想被外表评判了。

**参考译文**：
1. Understandings of beauty **vary from** culture **to** culture, and young people are especially easy to **be affected**.
2. **Instead of** chasing an **ideal** appearance, develop the ability **to make** you confident.
3. The girl **who wrote to the advice column** said she **no longer wanted to be judged by** her looks.

**采分点**：① vary from...to...；② instead of + 动名词；③ who 定语从句 + be judged by。

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 句 instead of 后接 to do（×）；第 3 句 no longer 位置错误。

**变式**：把第 2 句改为 "It is better to..." 句型：It is better to develop the ability that makes you confident than to chase an ideal appearance.

> 答案：① 名词后　② write 不及物，需介词接宾语　③ 事实可验证，观点含评价

## E-3.4 Travellers' tales

*必修三 Unit 4 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频中 ｜ 话题：旅行故事 ｜ 语法：-ed 形式作状语 ｜ 本单元 51 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："Seen from the top, the valley looks like a map." 中 Seen 的逻辑主语是谁？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讲"旅行者的故事"：读一篇关于"少有人走的路"的文章（A road less travelled），学 **-ed 形式作状语**，写一篇**旅行故事**。词汇集中在旅行、自然保护与个人感受。

### 30 秒自评

1. -ed 作状语时，主句主语是它的逻辑宾语吗？
2. "Tired, he sat down." 中 Tired 表示原因吗？
3. -ed 与 -ing 作状语的区别是什么？

> **答案**：1. 是（被动/完成关系）　2. 是　3. -ing 表主动/进行，-ed 表被动/完成

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **guideline** n. 指导原则：follow the **guidelines** 遵循指南；safety **guidelines** 安全准则
- **track** n./v. 小路；追踪：a mountain **track** 山间小路；**track** the animals 追踪动物
- **enthusiasm** n. 热情：with **enthusiasm** 热情地；**enthusiastic** adj. 热情的
- **conservation** n. 保护：wildlife **conservation** 野生动物保护；**conserve** v. 保护
- **escape** v./n. 逃离：**escape** from 从……逃离；a narrow **escape** 死里逃生
- **highlight** n./v. 亮点；突出：the **highlight** of the trip 旅行亮点；**highlight** the problem 突出问题
- **concept** n. 概念：the **concept** of  ……的概念；a new **concept** 新概念
- **specific** adj. 具体的：a **specific** example 具体例子；be **specific** about 对……具体说明
- **harm** n./v. 伤害：do **harm** to 对……有害；**harmful** adj. 有害的
- **engage** v. 参与；吸引：**engage** in 参与；**engage** the audience 吸引观众

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| set off | 出发 | We **set off** before sunrise. |
| be engaged in | 从事；参与 | She **is engaged in** a conservation project. |
| do harm to | 对……有害 | Tourism can **do harm to** the environment. |
| in search of | 寻找 | They travelled **in search of** a quieter beach. |
| escape from | 从……逃离 | The deer **escaped from** the fire. |
| be specific about | 对……具体 | Be **specific about** what you want to see. |
| highlight the importance of | 强调……的重要性 | The guide **highlighted the importance of** conservation. |
| look back on | 回顾 | I often **look back on** that journey. |

### 📐 语法：-ed 形式作状语

| 类型 | 例句 | 相当于 |
|---|---|---|
| 原因 | **Tired**, he sat down. | Because he was tired |
| 条件 | **Given** more time, we would stay. | If we were given more time |
| 时间 | **Seen** from above, the lake is blue. | When it is seen from above |
| 伴随 | He walked away, **followed by his dog**. | and was followed by his dog |

**要点**
1. -ed 状语与主句主语构成被动关系
2. 可扩展为 "being + done"（正在被）或 "having been done"（已经被）
3. 否定式 not done / not having been done

**易错边界**
1. 逻辑主语不一致：**Seen** from the top, the valley looks like a map.（√，valley 是被看的）／**Seeing** from the top, the valley...（×）
2. 该用 -ing 却用 -ed：**Walking** in the forest, I saw a deer.（√，I 主动走）
3. 完成式遗漏：**Having finished** the track, we rested.（√）

**三个练习**
1. ________ (give) a map, we found the way easily. → **Given**
2. ________ (follow) by a guide, the tourists felt safe. → **Followed**
3. 改错：Seeing from the hill, the city is beautiful. → **Seen from the hill, the city is beautiful.**

### ✍️ 写作：旅行故事（travel tale）

**结构**：出发（时间地点）→ 途中（2–3 个事件，含一个转折）→ 感受与收获

**五个可套用句式**
1. **It was a trip I will never forget.** 这是一次我永远不会忘记的旅行。
2. **We set off at dawn, ...** 我们黎明出发，……
3. **Just as we..., something unexpected happened.** 就在我们……时，意外发生了。
4. **Tired but excited, we...** 疲惫但兴奋，我们……
5. **Looking back, I realize that...** 回头看，我意识到……

### 📖 阅读技能：梳理游记时间线

游记按时间推进：先找**时间标志词**（at dawn, later, by noon, finally），再找**地点变化**（moved on to, arrived at）。转折处（but, however, suddenly）往往是故事重点，也是考题常设处。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：guideline / track / enthusiasm / conservation / escape

1. The hikers followed a narrow ______ up the mountain.
2. Wildlife ______ is important for the planet.
3. She talked about the trip with great ______.
4. The bird managed to ______ from the cage.
5. Please read the safety ______ before you start.

**解析**：1. track　2. conservation　3. enthusiasm　4. escape　5. guidelines

**💥 陷阱**：第 5 题 guideline 常用复数 guidelines。

**变式**：用 do harm to 造句，表达"乱扔垃圾会伤害野生动物"。→ Littering can do harm to wildlife.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用 -ed 形式改写：
1. Because he was tired, he stopped walking.
2. If we were given more time, we would explore the valley.
3. When it is seen from the top, the village looks tiny.

**解析**：
1. **Tired**, he stopped walking.
2. **Given** more time, we would explore the valley.
3. **Seen** from the top, the village looks tiny.

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题用 Seeing（主动）导致逻辑主语错误。

**变式**：用 "followed by" 造句：The guide walked ahead, followed by six tourists.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 沿着山间小路走，我们看到了许多在城市里见不到的鸟。
2. 受到当地人的热情鼓舞，我们决定多留两天。
3. 回头看这次旅行，我意识到真正的收获不是照片，而是对自然的新认识。

**参考译文**：
1. **Walking** along the mountain **track**, we saw many birds that cannot be seen in the city.
2. **Encouraged by** the local people's **enthusiasm**, we decided to stay two more days.
3. **Looking back on** the trip, I realize the real **gain** was not photos but a new understanding of nature.

**采分点**：① -ing 作状语 + 定语从句；② -ed 作状语（Encouraged by）；③ look back on + not...but...

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句用 Walked（被动）导致逻辑主语错误；第 2 句 encourage 后介词用成 with。

**变式**：把第 3 句改为强调句：It is not photos but a new understanding of nature that is the real gain.
