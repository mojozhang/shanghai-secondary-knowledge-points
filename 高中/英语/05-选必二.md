> 沪教版（2022）选择性必修第二册，4 个 Unit：Decisions / Digital humans / Creativity / Inspiration。

## E-5.1 Decisions

*选必二 Unit 1 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：选择与职业 ｜ 语法：动词不定式作主语和表语 ｜ 本单元 64 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："It is important to make a decision." 中 it 指代什么？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讲"人生选择"：读马克·吐温回忆"寻找真正职业"的节选（Finding one's true vocation），学**动词不定式作主语和表语**，写一封**求职自荐信（covering letter）**。词汇集中在职业、申请、资格与机构。

### 30 秒自评

1. 不定式作主语时，常用 it 作形式主语吗？
2. "My dream is to be a teacher." 中 to be 作什么成分？
3. "To see is to believe." 中两个不定式分别作什么？

> **答案**：1. 常用（It is + adj + to do）　2. 表语　3. 主语、表语

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **vocation** n. 职业；使命：find one's **vocation** 找到真正适合的职业；a sense of **vocation** 使命感
- **worthwhile** adj. 值得的：a **worthwhile** job 值得做的工作；It is **worthwhile** to do
- **deserve** v. 值得；应得：**deserve** to do 应该做；**deserve** attention 值得关注
- **passionate** adj. 热情的：be **passionate** about 对……充满热情；**passion** n. 热情
- **mission** n. 使命：a **mission** to do 做……的使命；on a **mission** 执行任务
- **insight** n. 洞察力：gain **insight** into 深入了解；**insightful** adj. 有洞察力的
- **counsellor** n. 顾问；辅导员：a career **counsellor** 职业顾问；**counsel** v. 建议
- **enthusiastic** adj. 热情的：be **enthusiastic** about 对……热心；**enthusiasm** n. 热情
- **candidate** n. 候选人；申请人：a **candidate** for ……的候选人；**candidacy** n. 候选资格
- **approve** v. 批准；赞成：**approve** of 赞成；**approval** n. 批准

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| find one's vocation | 找到真正的职业 | He finally **found his vocation** as a teacher. |
| be passionate about | 对……充满热情 | She **is passionate about** science. |
| gain insight into | 深入了解 | The interview gave me **insight into** the job. |
| apply for | 申请 | I **applied for** a scholarship. |
| be qualified for | 有资格做 | He **is qualified for** the position. |
| approve of | 赞成 | My parents **approve of** my choice. |
| make a living | 谋生 | She **makes a living** by writing. |
| think twice | 三思 | **Think twice** before you quit. |

### 📐 语法：动词不定式作主语和表语

| 用法 | 例句 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 主语 | **To find** a vocation takes time. | 不定式作主语，谓语用单数 |
| 形式主语 it | **It** takes time **to find** a vocation. | 常用，避免头重脚轻 |
| 表语 | My dream is **to become** a doctor. | 说明主语内容 |
| It is + adj + to do | **It is** hard **to choose**. | 常见句型 |

**要点**
1. 不定式作主语，谓语动词用单数：To learn languages **is** useful.
2. it 作形式主语的常见句型：It is + adj + (for/of sb) + to do
3. 表语用不定式时，主语常是 dream, aim, goal, plan, job 等

**易错边界**
1. 谓语用复数：To read books **are** helpful.（×）
2. for 与 of 混用：It is kind **for** you to help.（×，应为 of，形容人的品质）
3. 不定式与动名词作主语混淆：**Swimming** is good for health.（习惯）/ **To swim** today is impossible.（具体）

**三个练习**
1. ________ (learn) a skill takes patience. → **To learn**
2. It is important ________ (make) a plan first. → **to make**
3. 改错：It is generous for him to donate. → **It is generous of him to donate.**

### ✍️ 写作：求职自荐信（covering letter）

**结构**：称呼 → 申请职位与信息来源 → 2 个优势（经历/能力）→ 表达面试意愿 → 结尾

**五个可套用句式**
1. **I am writing to apply for the position of...** 我写信申请……职位。
2. **I graduated from... with a degree in...** 我毕业于……，获得……学位。
3. **I am passionate about... and have...** 我热爱……并且……
4. **I believe I am well qualified for this role because...** 我相信自己胜任这个岗位，因为……
5. **I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application.** 希望能有机会面谈。

### 📖 阅读技能：识别"对比与转折"

人物回忆类文章常写"当初以为……后来发现……"。抓住 **but, however, instead, in fact, what I really wanted** 等信号，就能找到作者态度转变的关键句。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：vocation / worthwhile / deserve / insight / approve

1. Teaching is a ______ job: it changes lives.
2. She finally found her ______ in medicine.
3. His advice gave me ______ into the industry.
4. Do you ______ of my decision?
5. Good work ______ to be recognized.

**解析**：1. worthwhile　2. vocation　3. insight　4. approve　5. deserves

**💥 陷阱**：第 5 题 deserve 后接 to be done 表示"应该被……"。

**变式**：用 be passionate about 造句，表达"她对教学充满热情"。→ She is passionate about teaching.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

改写句子（用形式主语 it）：
1. To choose a career is difficult.
2. To work with children is rewarding.
3. To learn from failure is important.

**解析**：
1. **It is difficult to choose** a career.
2. **It is rewarding to work** with children.
3. **It is important to learn** from failure.

**💥 陷阱**：改写后漏掉 it，写成 Is difficult to choose a career。

**变式**：用不定式作表语造句，表达"我的目标是成为一名记者"。→ My goal is to become a journalist.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 找到真正适合的职业需要时间，也需要勇气。
2. 他认为，值得做的工作不仅能谋生，还能带来成就感。
3. 我写信申请这个职位，因为我对教育充满热情，并且有两年教学经验。

**参考译文**：
1. **It takes** time and courage **to find** a **vocation** that really suits you.
2. He believes that a **worthwhile** job not only **makes a living** but also brings a sense of achievement.
3. I am writing **to apply for** the position because I **am passionate about** education and have two years' teaching experience.

**采分点**：① it takes + to do；② not only...but also；③ apply for + be passionate about。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句用 find 作主语但漏 it；第 3 句 apply 后漏 for。

**变式**：把第 2 句改为强调句：It is a worthwhile job that not only makes a living but also brings a sense of achievement.

### ⏱️ 限时挑战（60 秒）

> 答案：① 单数　② It is + adj + to do　③ apply for 申请职位；apply to 适用于

## E-5.2 Digital humans

*选必二 Unit 2 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：数字人与社交 ｜ 语法：非限制性定语从句 ｜ 本单元 62 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："My brother, that works in a tech company, loves robots." 这句话错在哪里？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"数字人类"：读一篇关于数字人一天的想象文（A day in the life of a digital human），学**非限制性定语从句**，写一篇**关于现代科技的正反观点文章**。词汇集中在网络身份、数据、隐私与情绪。

### 30 秒自评

1. 非限制性定语从句可以用 that 引导吗？
2. 非限制性定语从句与主句之间用什么隔开？
3. which 可以指代整个主句吗？

> **答案**：1. 不能　2. 逗号　3. 可以（He passed the exam, which surprised us.）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **status** n. 状态；地位：social **status** 社会地位；update the **status** 更新状态
- **analyse** v. 分析：**analyse** data 分析数据；**analysis** n. 分析
- **compromise** n./v. 妥协：reach a **compromise** 达成妥协；**compromise** on 在……上妥协
- **profile** n. 个人资料：a social media **profile** 社交媒体主页；keep a low **profile** 保持低调
- **delete** v. 删除：**delete** a post 删除帖子；**delete** the file 删除文件
- **sympathetic** adj. 同情的：be **sympathetic** to 对……同情；**sympathy** n. 同情
- **potential** adj./n. 潜在的；潜力：**potential** risk 潜在风险；reach one's **potential** 发挥潜力
- **dare** v. 敢于：**dare** to do 敢于做；**dare** not do 不敢做
- **absorb** v. 吸收；使全神贯注：**absorb** information 吸收信息；be **absorbed** in 专注于
- **objective** adj./n. 客观的；目标：an **objective** view 客观看法；achieve an **objective** 实现目标

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be absorbed in | 专注于 | He **was absorbed in** his phone. |
| reach a compromise | 达成妥协 | Both sides **reached a compromise**. |
| be sympathetic to | 同情 | She **was sympathetic to** his situation. |
| be aware of | 意识到 | Users should **be aware of** the risks. |
| take control of | 控制 | **Take control of** your online time. |
| in terms of | 在……方面 | **In terms of** privacy, the app is weak. |
| have the potential to | 有潜力做 | AI **has the potential to** change work. |
| keep a low profile | 保持低调 | He **keeps a low profile** online. |

### 📐 语法：非限制性定语从句

| 关系词 | 指代 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| who | 人 | My teacher, **who** is from Canada, speaks Chinese. |
| whom | 人（宾语） | Her brother, **whom** I met yesterday, is a doctor. |
| which | 物/整句 | The app, **which** was launched last year, is popular. |
| whose | 所属 | The company, **whose** products are famous, is in Shenzhen. |

**要点**
1. 用逗号与主句隔开，**不能用 that**
2. which 可指代整个主句：He lied, **which** made us angry.
3. 非限制性从句删去后，主句意思仍完整

**易错边界**
1. 用 that：My phone, **that** I bought last week, is broken.（×）
2. 逗号位置错误：My phone that I bought last week, is broken.（×）
3. 关系词省略：My phone, I bought last week, is broken.（×，非限制性不能省）

**三个练习**
1. The scientist, ________ research changed medicine, won the prize. → **whose**
2. He arrived late, ________ made his teacher angry. → **which**
3. 改错：My friend, that lives in Beijing, is a writer. → **who**

### ✍️ 写作：正反观点文章（科技）

**结构**：引出话题 → 支持科技的 2 个理由 → 反对/担忧的 2 个理由 → 自己的平衡观点

**五个可套用句式**
1. **Technology has changed the way we...** 科技改变了我们……的方式。
2. **On the one hand, ...; on the other hand, ...** 一方面……；另一方面……
3. **Supporters argue that...** 支持者认为……
4. **However, critics worry that...** 然而，批评者担心……
5. **In conclusion, we should... rather than...** 总之，我们应该……而不是……

### 📖 阅读技能：识别例证与数据

说明文中，**for example, such as, according to** 后是例证；数字与百分比是论据。做推理题时，先区分"事实"与"作者评价"，不要把自己的常识当成作者观点。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：status / analyse / compromise / profile / delete

1. Please ______ the photo from your phone.
2. The app can ______ your data and give advice.
3. They reached a ______ after a long talk.
4. His social media ______ shows only happy moments.
5. The ______ of the project is "in progress".

**解析**：1. delete　2. analyse　3. compromise　4. profile　5. status

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题 analyse 是英式拼写，美式为 analyze，均正确。

**变式**：用 be absorbed in 造句，表达"他专注于手机，没听见我说话"。→ He was absorbed in his phone and didn't hear me.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

把下列句子改为非限制性定语从句：
1. My uncle is a doctor. He lives in Shanghai.
2. The app is popular. It was launched last year.
3. She failed the exam. This surprised everyone.

**解析**：
1. My uncle, **who lives in Shanghai**, is a doctor.
2. The app, **which was launched last year**, is popular.
3. She failed the exam, **which surprised everyone**.

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 句用 that 引导（×）；第 3 句用 what 指代整句（×）。

**变式**：用 whose 合并："The company is in Shenzhen. Its products are famous." → The company, whose products are famous, is in Shenzhen.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 这款应用可以分析你的数据，这引发了人们对隐私的担忧。
2. 一方面，数字人可以帮助我们工作；另一方面，它们也可能取代一些岗位。
3. 我们应该学会控制上网时间，而不是被手机控制。

**参考译文**：
1. The app can **analyse** your data, **which** has raised concerns about privacy.
2. **On the one hand**, digital humans can help us work; **on the other hand**, they may **replace** some jobs.
3. We should learn to **take control of** our screen time **rather than** be controlled by our phones.

**采分点**：① which 指代整句；② on the one hand...on the other hand；③ rather than + 动词原形。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句用 that 指代整句（×）；第 3 句 rather than 后接 to be controlled（×）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为两个简单句：The app can analyse your data. This has raised concerns about privacy.

> 答案：① 不能　② 单数　③ 专注于
