## E-2.1 No limits

*必修二 Unit 1 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：极限与冒险 ｜ 语法：省略 ｜ 本单元 47 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："While reading, the book was interesting." 这句话对吗？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"极限"：读一篇解释冒险心理的文章（Blame your brain），学**省略（ellipsis）**，写一篇**描述你敬佩的人**的文章。词汇集中在风险、判断、自信与成就。

### 30 秒自评

1. 状语从句省略时，从句主语与主句主语必须一致吗？
2. "When (he was) young, he climbed the mountain." 是省略用法吗？
3. 不定式省略时，保留 to 吗？

> **答案**：1. 是（否则不能省）　2. 是（省略 he was）　3. 通常保留（I want to.）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **blame** v./n. 责备；归咎：**blame** somebody for 因……责怪；be to **blame** 应受责备
- **factor** n. 因素：a key **factor** 关键因素；**factor** in 把……考虑在内
- **pressure** n. 压力：under **pressure** 在压力下；**pressurize** v. 施压
- **impress** v. 使印象深刻：be **impressed** by 对……印象深刻；**impressive** adj. 令人印象深刻的
- **independent** adj. 独立的：be **independent** of 不依赖；**independence** n. 独立
- **adapt** v. 适应；改编：**adapt** to 适应；**adapt** a novel 改编小说
- **reveal** v. 揭示：**reveal** the truth 揭示真相；**revealing** adj. 发人深省的
- **responsible** adj. 负责的：be **responsible** for 对……负责；**responsibility** n. 责任
- **strategy** n. 策略：a **strategy** for doing 做……的策略；**strategic** adj. 战略的
- **confidence** n. 自信：have **confidence** in 对……有信心；**confident** adj. 自信的

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be to blame | 应受责备 | The driver **was to blame** for the accident. |
| under pressure | 在压力下 | He made the decision **under pressure**. |
| be impressed by | 对……印象深刻 | We **were impressed by** her courage. |
| adapt to | 适应 | It takes time to **adapt to** a new school. |

### 📐 语法：省略（ellipsis）

**五种常见省略**

| 类型 | 完整 | 省略后 |
|---|---|---|
| 简单句 | (I) Beg your pardon. | Beg your pardon. |
| 并列句 | I like tea and (I like) coffee. | I like tea and coffee. |
| 状语从句 | When (he was) young, he skied. | When young, he skied. |
| 定语从句 | the book (that) I bought | the book I bought |
| 不定式 | I want to go, but I can't (go). | I want to go, but I can't. |

**要点**
1. 状语从句省略：主语与主句一致，且从句谓语含 be 动词时，可省"主语 + be"
2. 不定式省略：保留 to（I'd like to.），be 动词/助动词常保留
3. 省略不能造成歧义

**易错边界**
1. 主语不一致仍省略：While reading, the book was interesting.（×，reading 的逻辑主语不是 book）
2. 不定式省略漏 to：I didn't want to, but I had to.（√，had to 后省 go）
3. 状语从句省略后连词后直接跟过去分词，逻辑主语错误：When seen from the hill, the city looks beautiful.（√，city 是 seen 的逻辑主语）

**三个练习**
1. While ________ (wait) for the bus, I met an old friend. → **waiting**
2. He didn't come, though ________ (invite). → **invited**
3. 改错：Although tired, but he kept working. → 去掉 but

### ✍️ 写作：描述你敬佩的人

**结构**：点明人物与关系 → 2 个具体事例（体现品质）→ 影响与评价

**五个可套用句式**
1. **The person I admire most is...** 我最敬佩的人是……
2. **What impresses me most is his/her...** 最让我印象深刻的是他/她的……
3. **Once, he/she...** 有一次，他/她……
4. **Thanks to him/her, I learned that...** 多亏了他/她，我懂得了……
5. **He/She is a person who...** 他/她是一个……的人。

### 📖 阅读技能：找段落主旨

段首句常是主旨；若段首是例子，主旨多在段尾。先划出每段首尾句，再用 5 个字概括每段，串起来就是全文结构。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：blame / pressure / adapt / reveal / confidence

1. Don't ______ others for your own mistakes.
2. She has great ______ in her own ability.
3. It took him a month to ______ to the new job.
4. The survey ______ that most teenagers feel ______ from exams.

**解析**：1. blame　2. confidence　3. adapt　4. revealed；pressure

**💥 陷阱**：第 4 题第二个空易填 pressuring，应为名词 pressure。

**变式**：用 be responsible for 造句，表达"大脑负责我们的风险评估"。→ The brain is responsible for our risk assessment.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

改写为省略句：
1. When he was young, he began to ski.
2. If it is possible, we will start tomorrow.
3. She didn't come, although she was invited.

**解析**：
1. **When young**, he began to ski.
2. **If possible**, we will start tomorrow.
3. She didn't come, **although invited**.

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题省略后仍保留 she was，写成 although she invited（被动变主动）。

**变式**：把 "While I was walking in the park, I saw a fox." 改为省略句。→ While walking in the park, I saw a fox.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 面对压力时，他总能保持自信并迅速适应。
2. 调查显示，冒险并不总是鲁莽，关键在于判断。
3. 我最敬佩的人是那位从不把失败归咎于别人的老师。

**参考译文**：
1. **In the face of pressure**, he can always stay **confident** and **adapt** quickly.
2. The survey **reveals** that **taking risks** is not always reckless; the key lies in **judgement**.
3. The person I admire most is the teacher **who never blames others for his failure**.

**采分点**：① in the face of + stay confident；② reveal that + 动名词作主语；③ who 定语从句 + blame sb for。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句 adapt 后漏 to；第 3 句 blame 后介词用成 on。

**变式**：把第 2 句改为 "It is...that..." 强调句。→ It is judgement that matters most in taking risks.

## E-2.2 Roads to education

*必修二 Unit 2 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：教育之路 ｜ 语法：-ing 与 -ed 形式作定语 ｜ 本单元 58 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："I am boring in this class." 和 "This class is boring." 哪句有问题？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"教育"：读一篇关于教育实验的文章（An experiment in education），学 **-ing 与 -ed 形式作定语**，写一篇**正反观点文章（for and against essay）**。词汇集中在课堂、讲座、动机与学习效果。

### 30 秒自评

1. a bored student 和 a boring student 意思一样吗？
2. -ed 形容词通常修饰"人"，-ing 形容词通常修饰"物"吗？
3. "a developing country" 和 "a developed country" 意思相同吗？

> **答案**：1. 不一样（感到无聊的学生 / 令人无聊的学生）　2. 大体如此（也有例外，如 a frightened look）　3. 不同（发展中国家 / 发达国家）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **episode** n. 一集；一段经历：the latest **episode** 最新一集；a painful **episode** 痛苦的经历
- **previous** adj. 以前的：the **previous** year 前一年；**previously** adv. 先前
- **positive** adj. 积极的；肯定的：a **positive** attitude 积极态度；**positive** effect 正面影响
- **effect** n. 影响；效果：have an **effect** on 对……有影响；side **effect** 副作用
- **motivate** v. 激励：**motivate** students to do 激励学生做；**motivation** n. 动机
- **audience** n. 观众；听众：a large **audience** 大量观众；target **audience** 目标受众
- **benefit** n./v. 益处；受益：**benefit** from 从……受益；be of **benefit** to 对……有益
- **cooperation** n. 合作：in **cooperation** with 与……合作；**cooperate** v. 合作
- **academic** adj. 学业的；学术的：**academic** performance 学业表现；**academic** year 学年
- **prevent** v. 阻止：**prevent** somebody from doing 阻止某人做；**prevention** n. 预防

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| have an effect on | 对……有影响 | Music **has a positive effect on** mood. |
| motivate somebody to do | 激励某人做 | Good teachers **motivate** us **to** learn. |
| benefit from | 从……受益 | Students **benefit from** small classes. |
| in cooperation with | 与……合作 | The school works **in cooperation with** a university. |
| prevent somebody from doing | 阻止某人做 | Nothing can **prevent** him **from** trying. |
| be based on | 以……为基础 | The course **is based on** real projects. |
| take part in | 参加 | Everyone **took part in** the experiment. |
| in terms of | 在……方面 | **In terms of** results, the class improved. |

### 📐 语法：-ing 与 -ed 形式作定语

| 形式 | 含义 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| -ing（主动/进行） | 令人……的；正在……的 | a **boring** lecture 无聊的讲座；a **developing** country 发展中国家 |
| -ed（被动/完成） | 感到……的；已经……的 | a **bored** student 感到无聊的学生；a **developed** country 发达国家 |

**要点**
1. 修饰人常用 -ed（感到），修饰物常用 -ing（令人），但 **a frightened look**（人脸上"感到害怕的"表情）也常见
2. 不及物动词的 -ing 表进行：the **rising** sun；-ed 表完成：the **risen** sun（较少用）
3. 短语作定语要后置：the students **sitting in the front row**

**易错边界**
1. I am boring.（×，应为 bored；除非"我这个人很无趣"）
2. a developing country 与 a developed country 混用
3. -ing 短语作定语位置错误：the students sitting there（√，后置）；the sitting there students（×）

**三个练习**
1. The lecture was so ________ (bore) that half the audience fell asleep. → **boring**
2. ________ (interest) in the topic, she read three books about it. → **Interested**
3. 改错：China is a developed country. → **developing**

### ✍️ 写作：正反观点文章（for and against）

**结构**：引入话题（1 句）→ 支持方 2 个理由 → 反对方 2 个理由 → 自己观点（1–2 句）

**五个可套用句式**
1. **Opinions are divided on whether...** 关于是否……意见不一。
2. **Those who support it argue that...** 支持者认为……
3. **On the other hand, critics point out that...** 另一方面，批评者指出……
4. **In my view, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks.** 在我看来，利大于弊。
5. **A balance should be struck between...** 应在……之间取得平衡。

### 📖 阅读技能：识别作者态度

注意形容词、副词与转折词：**fortunately, unfortunately, surprisingly, however, yet**。作者说 "The results were, surprisingly, positive." 态度是"意外但肯定"。选项中常设"中立/支持/反对"三类。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：episode / positive / motivate / benefit / prevent

1. The new ______ of the series is about a school experiment.
2. Praise can ______ students to work harder.
3. Regular exercise has a ______ effect on health.
4. We should ______ accidents by following the rules.
5. Both sides ______ from the cooperation.

**解析**：1. episode　2. motivate　3. positive　4. prevent　5. benefit

**💥 陷阱**：第 5 题 benefit 作动词，主语 Both sides 后直接跟 from，不加 s。

**变式**：用 have an effect on 造句，表达"教师的态度影响学生的动机"。→ Teachers' attitudes have an effect on students' motivation.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用括号中词的正确形式填空：
1. The ________ (excite) students waited for the result.
2. It was an ________ (excite) match.
3. The ________ (confuse) instructions made everyone lost.
4. She looked ________ (confuse).

**解析**：1. excited　2. exciting　3. confusing　4. confused

**💥 陷阱**：第 3、4 题一个修饰物、一个修饰人，形式相反。

**变式**：用 "a developing country" 造句，表达"发展中国家需要更多教育资源"。→ A developing country needs more educational resources.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 这项教育实验表明，小组合作对学生的学业表现有积极影响。
2. 被这个讲座激励的学生决定成立一个读书俱乐部。
3. 尽管有人认为考试会阻碍学习，我仍然认为它有助于发现问题和预防更大的失败。

**参考译文**：
1. The education **experiment** shows that **cooperation** in groups **has a positive effect on** students' **academic** performance.
2. The students **motivated by** the **lecture** decided to set up a reading club.
3. Although some believe exams **prevent** learning, I still think they help **discover** problems and **prevent** bigger **failures**.

**采分点**：① show that + have an effect on；② 过去分词短语作定语；③ although 让步 + prevent...from 的省略形式。

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 句用 motivating（主动）修饰 students（应为被动"被激励的"）；第 3 句 prevent 后漏 from 或误加 from 后不接动名词。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为被动语态。→ It is shown by the experiment that cooperation in groups has a positive effect on students' academic performance.
