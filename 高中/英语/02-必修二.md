## E-2.1 No limits

*必修二 Unit 1 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：极限与冒险 ｜ 语法：省略 ｜ 本单元 47 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："While reading, the book was interesting." 这句话对吗？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"极限"：读一篇解释冒险心理的文章（Blame your brain），学**省略（ellipsis）**，写一篇**描述你敬佩的人**的文章。词汇集中在风险、判断、自信与成就。

### 30 秒自评

1. 状语从句省略时，从句主语与主句主语必须一致吗？
2. "When (he was) young, he climbed the mountain." 是省略用法吗？
3. 不定式省略时，保留 to 吗？

> **答案**：1. 是（否则不能省）　2. 是（省略 he was）　3. 通常保留（I want to.）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **blame** v./n. 责备；归咎：**blame** somebody for 因……责怪；be to **blame** 应受责备
- **factor** n. 因素：a key **factor** 关键因素；**factor** in 把……考虑在内
- **pressure** n. 压力：under **pressure** 在压力下；**pressurize** v. 施压
- **impress** v. 使印象深刻：be **impressed** by 对……印象深刻；**impressive** adj. 令人印象深刻的
- **independent** adj. 独立的：be **independent** of 不依赖；**independence** n. 独立
- **adapt** v. 适应；改编：**adapt** to 适应；**adapt** a novel 改编小说
- **reveal** v. 揭示：**reveal** the truth 揭示真相；**revealing** adj. 发人深省的
- **responsible** adj. 负责的：be **responsible** for 对……负责；**responsibility** n. 责任
- **strategy** n. 策略：a **strategy** for doing 做……的策略；**strategic** adj. 战略的
- **confidence** n. 自信：have **confidence** in 对……有信心；**confident** adj. 自信的

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |

| under pressure | 在压力下 | He made the decision **under pressure**. |

### 📐 语法：省略（ellipsis）

**五种常见省略**

| 类型 | 完整 | 省略后 |
|---|---|---|
| 简单句 | (I) Beg your pardon. | Beg your pardon. |
| 并列句 | I like tea and (I like) coffee. | I like tea and coffee. |
| 状语从句 | When (he was) young, he skied. | When young, he skied. |
| 定语从句 | the book (that) I bought | the book I bought |
| 不定式 | I want to go, but I can't (go). | I want to go, but I can't. |

**要点**

1. 状语从句省略：主语与主句一致，且从句谓语含 be 动词时，可省"主语 + be"
2. 不定式省略：保留 to（I'd like to.），be 动词/助动词常保留
3. 省略不能造成歧义

**易错边界**

1. 主语不一致仍省略：While reading, the book was interesting.（×，reading 的逻辑主语不是 book）
2. 不定式省略漏 to：I didn't want to, but I had to.（√，had to 后省 go）
3. 状语从句省略后连词后直接跟过去分词，逻辑主语错误：When seen from the hill, the city looks beautiful.（√，city 是 seen 的逻辑主语）

**三个练习**

1. While ________ (wait) for the bus, I met an old friend. → **waiting**
2. He didn't come, though ________ (invite). → **invited**
3. 改错：Although tired, but he kept working. → 去掉 but

### ✍️ 写作：描述你敬佩的人

**结构**：点明人物与关系 → 2 个具体事例（体现品质）→ 影响与评价

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：blame / pressure / adapt / reveal / confidence

1. Don't ______ others for your own mistakes.
2. She has great ______ in her own ability.
3. It took him a month to ______ to the new job.
4. The survey ______ that most teenagers feel ______ from exams.

**解析**：1. blame　2. confidence　3. adapt　4. revealed；pressure

**💥 陷阱**：第 4 题第二个空易填 pressuring，应为名词 pressure。

**变式**：用 be responsible for 造句，表达"大脑负责我们的风险评估"。→ The brain is responsible for our risk assessment.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

改写为省略句：

1. When he was young, he began to ski.
2. If it is possible, we will start tomorrow.
3. She didn't come, although she was invited.

**解析**：

1. **When young**, he began to ski.
2. **If possible**, we will start tomorrow.
3. She didn't come, **although invited**.

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题省略后仍保留 she was，写成 although she invited（被动变主动）。

**变式**：把 "While I was walking in the park, I saw a fox." 改为省略句。→ While walking in the park, I saw a fox.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 面对压力时，他总能保持自信并迅速适应。
2. 调查显示，冒险并不总是鲁莽，关键在于判断。
3. 我最敬佩的人是那位从不把失败归咎于别人的老师。

**参考译文**：

1. **In the face of pressure**, he can always stay **confident** and **adapt** quickly.
2. The survey **reveals** that **taking risks** is not always reckless; the key lies in **judgement**.
3. The person I admire most is the teacher **who never blames others for his failure**.

**采分点**：① in the face of + stay confident；② reveal that + 动名词作主语；③ who 定语从句 + blame sb for。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句 adapt 后漏 to；第 3 句 blame 后介词用成 on。

**变式**：把第 2 句改为 "It is...that..." 强调句。→ It is judgement that matters most in taking risks.

## E-2.2 Roads to education

*必修二 Unit 2 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：教育之路 ｜ 语法：-ing 与 -ed 形式作定语 ｜ 本单元 58 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："I am boring in this class." 和 "This class is boring." 哪句有问题？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"教育"：读一篇关于教育实验的文章（An experiment in education），学 **-ing 与 -ed 形式作定语**，写一篇**正反观点文章（for and against essay）**。词汇集中在课堂、讲座、动机与学习效果。

### 30 秒自评

1. a bored student 和 a boring student 意思一样吗？
2. -ed 形容词通常修饰"人"，-ing 形容词通常修饰"物"吗？
3. "a developing country" 和 "a developed country" 意思相同吗？

> **答案**：1. 不一样（感到无聊的学生 / 令人无聊的学生）　2. 大体如此（也有例外，如 a frightened look）　3. 不同（发展中国家 / 发达国家）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **episode** n. 一集；一段经历：the latest **episode** 最新一集；a painful **episode** 痛苦的经历
- **previous** adj. 以前的：the **previous** year 前一年；**previously** adv. 先前
- **positive** adj. 积极的；肯定的：a **positive** attitude 积极态度；**positive** effect 正面影响
- **effect** n. 影响；效果：have an **effect** on 对……有影响；side **effect** 副作用
- **motivate** v. 激励：**motivate** students to do 激励学生做；**motivation** n. 动机
- **audience** n. 观众；听众：a large **audience** 大量观众；target **audience** 目标受众
- **benefit** n./v. 益处；受益：**benefit** from 从……受益；be of **benefit** to 对……有益
- **cooperation** n. 合作：in **cooperation** with 与……合作；**cooperate** v. 合作
- **academic** adj. 学业的；学术的：**academic** performance 学业表现；**academic** year 学年
- **prevent** v. 阻止：**prevent** somebody from doing 阻止某人做；**prevention** n. 预防

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| have an effect on | 对……有影响 | Music **has a positive effect on** mood. |
| motivate somebody to do | 激励某人做 | Good teachers **motivate** us **to** learn. |
| benefit from | 从……受益 | Students **benefit from** small classes. |
| in cooperation with | 与……合作 | The school works **in cooperation with** a university. |
| prevent somebody from doing | 阻止某人做 | Nothing can **prevent** him **from** trying. |
| be based on | 以……为基础 | The course **is based on** real projects. |
| take part in | 参加 | Everyone **took part in** the experiment. |
| in terms of | 在……方面 | **In terms of** results, the class improved. |

### 📐 语法：-ing 与 -ed 形式作定语

| 形式 | 含义 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| -ing（主动/进行） | 令人……的；正在……的 | a **boring** lecture 无聊的讲座；a **developing** country 发展中国家 |
| -ed（被动/完成） | 感到……的；已经……的 | a **bored** student 感到无聊的学生；a **developed** country 发达国家 |

**要点**

1. 修饰人常用 -ed（感到），修饰物常用 -ing（令人），但 **a frightened look**（人脸上"感到害怕的"表情）也常见
2. 不及物动词的 -ing 表进行：the **rising** sun；-ed 表完成：the **risen** sun（较少用）
3. 短语作定语要后置：the students **sitting in the front row**

**易错边界**

1. I am boring.（×，应为 bored；除非"我这个人很无趣"）
2. a developing country 与 a developed country 混用
3. -ing 短语作定语位置错误：the students sitting there（√，后置）；the sitting there students（×）

**三个练习**

1. The lecture was so ________ (bore) that half the audience fell asleep. → **boring**
2. ________ (interest) in the topic, she read three books about it. → **Interested**
3. 改错：China is a developed country. → **developing**

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：episode / positive / motivate / benefit / prevent

1. The new ______ of the series is about a school experiment.
2. Praise can ______ students to work harder.
3. Regular exercise has a ______ effect on health.
4. We should ______ accidents by following the rules.
5. Both sides ______ from the cooperation.

**解析**：1. episode　2. motivate　3. positive　4. prevent　5. benefit

**💥 陷阱**：第 5 题 benefit 作动词，主语 Both sides 后直接跟 from，不加 s。

**变式**：用 have an effect on 造句，表达"教师的态度影响学生的动机"。→ Teachers' attitudes have an effect on students' motivation.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用括号中词的正确形式填空：

1. The ________ (excite) students waited for the result.
2. It was an ________ (excite) match.
3. The ________ (confuse) instructions made everyone lost.
4. She looked ________ (confuse).

**解析**：1. excited　2. exciting　3. confusing　4. confused

**💥 陷阱**：第 3、4 题一个修饰物、一个修饰人，形式相反。

**变式**：用 "a developing country" 造句，表达"发展中国家需要更多教育资源"。→ A developing country needs more educational resources.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 这项教育实验表明，小组合作对学生的学业表现有积极影响。
2. 被这个讲座激励的学生决定成立一个读书俱乐部。
3. 尽管有人认为考试会阻碍学习，我仍然认为它有助于发现问题和预防更大的失败。

**参考译文**：

1. The education **experiment** shows that **cooperation** in groups **has a positive effect on** students' **academic** performance.
2. The students **motivated by** the **lecture** decided to set up a reading club.
3. Although some believe exams **prevent** learning, I still think they help **discover** problems and **prevent** bigger **failures**.

**采分点**：① show that + have an effect on；② 过去分词短语作定语；③ although 让步 + prevent...from 的省略形式。

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 句用 motivating（主动）修饰 students（应为被动"被激励的"）；第 3 句 prevent 后漏 from 或误加 from 后不接动名词。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为被动语态。→ It is shown by the experiment that cooperation in groups has a positive effect on students' academic performance.

## E-2.3 Progress?

*必修二 Unit 3 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频中 ｜ 话题：全球化与进步 ｜ 语法：-ing 形式作状语 ｜ 本单元 52 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："Walking down the street, the shops attracted me." 这句话有什么问题？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"进步"：读一篇关于全球化的文章（Going global），学 **-ing 形式作状语**，写一封**咨询课程的正式邮件**。词汇集中在气候、数据、农业与选择。

### 30 秒自评

1. -ing 形式作状语时，它的逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致吗？
2. "Having finished his homework, he went out." 表示动作先后吗？
3. 结果状语可以用 -ing 形式吗？

> **答案**：1. 必须一致　2. 是（完成作业在先）　3. 可以（He fell, breaking his leg.）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **climate** n. 气候：**climate** change 气候变化；a mild **climate** 温和的气候
- **alarming** adj. 令人担忧的：an **alarming** rate 惊人的速度；**alarm** v./n. 警报
- **rate** n. 比率；速度：at a **rate** of 以……的速度；birth **rate** 出生率
- **predict** v. 预测：**predict** the future 预测未来；**prediction** n. 预测
- **agriculture** n. 农业：modern **agriculture** 现代农业；**agricultural** adj. 农业的

- **reduce** v. 减少：**reduce** by 减少（幅度）；**reduce** to 减少到

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| at an alarming rate | 以惊人的速度 | Forests are disappearing **at an alarming rate**. |
| predict the future | 预测未来 | No one can **predict the future** exactly. |
| face extinction | 面临灭绝 | Many species **face extinction**. |
| reduce... by/to | 减少……／到…… | We must **reduce** waste **by** half. |
| have no option but to do | 别无选择只能 | They **had no option but to** leave. |
| graduate from | 毕业于 | She **graduated from** a college in Shanghai. |
| raise funds for | 为……筹款 | Students **raised funds for** the project. |
| rely on | 依赖 | Farming **relies on** the weather. |

### 📐 语法：-ing 形式作状语

| 类型 | 例句 | 相当于 |
|---|---|---|
| 时间 | **Walking** home, I met an old friend. | When I was walking home |
| 原因 | **Being** ill, he didn't come. | Because he was ill |
| 条件 | **Working** hard, you will succeed. | If you work hard |
| 结果 | He fell, **breaking** his leg. | and broke his leg |
| 伴随 | She sat by the window, **reading**. | and read |

**要点**

1. 逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致
2. 完成式 having done 表示动作发生在主句动作之前
3. 否定式 not doing / not having done

**易错边界**

1. 逻辑主语不一致：**Walking** down the street, the shops attracted me.（×，应为 I was attracted...）
2. 完成式与一般式混用：**Finishing** his work, he went home.（应为 Having finished，强调先后）
3. 否定形式错误：**Not** having money, he...（√）；Having not money（×）

**三个练习**

1. ________ (see) from the hill, the city looks beautiful. → **Seen**（注意：此处用过去分词，因 city 被看）
2. ________ (not know) the answer, she kept silent. → **Not knowing**
3. 改错：Having finished the report, the meeting began. → **Having finished the report, he left for the meeting.**（逻辑主语一致）

### ✍️ 写作：正式咨询邮件（formal email of enquiry）

**结构**：称呼（Dear Sir/Madam）→ 说明来意 → 具体问题（2–3 个）→ 期待回复 → 署名

**五个可套用句式**

1. **I am writing to enquire about...** 我写信是想咨询……
2. **I would be grateful if you could tell me...** 如能告知……我将不胜感激。
3. **Could you please let me know whether...?** 能否告知是否……？
4. **I would also like to know...** 我还想知道……
5. **I look forward to hearing from you.** 期待您的回复。

### 📖 阅读技能：读数字与趋势

看到数字先判断它说明"数量、比例还是速度"；注意 by / to / from / to 的区别（increase **by** 10% 增长了 10%；increase **to** 10% 增长到 10%）。图表题先看单位与时间轴。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：climate / rate / predict / reduce / option

1. Scientists cannot ______ earthquakes exactly.
2. The birth ______ has fallen in recent years.
3. We have no ______ but to wait.
4. We should ______ the use of plastic.
5. ______ change is a global problem.

**解析**：1. predict　2. rate　3. option　4. reduce　5. Climate

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题 have no option but to do 中 but 后接带 to 的不定式。

**变式**：用 at an alarming rate 造句，表达"城市以惊人的速度扩张"。→ Cities are expanding at an alarming rate.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用 -ing 形式改写句子：

1. Because he was tired, he went to bed early.
2. After she finished the work, she went shopping.
3. If you turn left, you will see the museum.

**解析**：

1. **Being tired**, he went to bed early.
2. **Having finished the work**, she went shopping.
3. **Turning left**, you will see the museum.

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题用 Finishing（一般式），未体现"完成在先"。

**变式**：用 -ing 形式合并："He sat by the window. He read a book." → He sat by the window, reading a book.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 森林正以惊人的速度消失，许多物种因此面临灭绝。
2. 为了减少污染，政府别无选择，只能资助公共交通。
3. 看到这些数据，我们意识到必须立即采取行动。

**参考译文**：

1. Forests are disappearing **at an alarming rate**, and many species **face extinction** as a result.
2. To **reduce** pollution, the government **had no option but to** **fund** public transport.
3. **Seeing** these figures, we realized that we must take action **immediately**.

**采分点**：① at an alarming rate + face extinction；② have no option but to do；③ -ing 作状语 + realize that。

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 句 Seeing 的逻辑主语与主句不一致（应为 we）；第 2 句 but 后漏 to。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为定语从句：Forests, which are disappearing at an alarming rate, are home to many species facing extinction.

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **gain** v./n. 获得；增加：**gain** experience 获得经验；**gain** weight 增重
- **attract** v. 吸引：**attract** attention 吸引注意；**attractive** adj. 有吸引力的
- **target** n./v. 目标；以……为目标：hit the **target** 命中目标；be **targeted** at 针对
- **approach** n./v. 方法；接近：a new **approach** to 新方法；**approach** the problem 处理问题
- **defeat** v./n. 击败；失败：**defeat** the enemy 打败敌人；admit **defeat** 认输
- **strength** n. 力量；长处：physical **strength** 体力；**strengths** and weaknesses 优缺点
- **struggle** v./n. 奋斗；挣扎：**struggle** to do 努力做；**struggle** with 与……斗争
- **maintain** v. 维持；保养：**maintain** balance 保持平衡；**maintain** that 坚持认为
- **outstanding** adj. 杰出的：an **outstanding** achievement 杰出成就；**outstanding** performance 出色表现
- **realistic** adj. 现实的：a **realistic** goal 现实的目标；be **realistic** about 对……现实

### 📐 语法：动词不定式作状语

| 类型 | 例句 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 目的 | He got up early **to catch** the bus. | = in order to / so as to |
| 结果 | He studied hard, **only to fail**. | 出乎意料的结果 |
| 结果 | She is old enough **to vote**. | enough to |
| 结果 | He is too tired **to walk**. | too...to（含否定） |
| 原因 | I'm glad **to see** you. | 情绪形容词后 |

**要点**

1. 目的状语：to do / in order to do / so as to do（so as to 不放句首）
2. 结果状语：only to do（意外结果）；enough to / too...to / so...as to
3. 原因状语：be glad / sorry / surprised to do

**易错边界**

1. 目的状语用 for doing：I came here for seeing you.（×，应用 to see）
2. only to 与 only doing 混用：He fell, only to break his leg.（结果用 to do）
3. too...to 后加 not：too tired not to walk（×，too...to 本身含否定）

**三个练习**

1. He hurried to the station, only ________ (find) the train had left. → **to find**
2. She is old enough ________ (make) her own decision. → **to make**
3. 改错：I'm sorry for hear the news. → **I'm sorry to hear the news.**

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：gain / attract / approach / strength / maintain

1. The exhibition ______ thousands of visitors last year.
2. We need a new ______ to teaching grammar.
3. He used all his ______ to lift the box.
4. It is hard to ______ such a high speed.
5. You will ______ a lot from this experience.

**解析**：1. attracted　2. approach　3. strength　4. maintain　5. gain

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题 attract 的过去式双写 t（attracted 不双写；注意与 planned 类区分）。

**变式**：用 be proud of 造句，表达"我们为他的坚持感到自豪"。→ We are proud of his persistence.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用不定式完成句子：

1. He got up early ________ (catch) the first train.
2. She was too excited ________ (say) a word.
3. I'm glad ________ (meet) you here.

**解析**：1. **to catch**（目的）　2. **to say**（too...to 含否定）　3. **to meet**（原因）

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题误加 not，写成 not to say。

**变式**：用 only to 改写："He tried to open the door, but found it locked." → He tried to open the door, only to find it locked.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 那位老人与鲨鱼搏斗了几个小时，最终只带回一副鱼骨。
2. 为了保持平衡，他不得不用尽全身力气抓住鱼线。
3. 虽然他没有赢得比赛，但他的坚持赢得了所有人的尊重。

**参考译文**：

1. The old man **struggled with** the shark for hours, **only to** bring back a skeleton.
2. **To maintain** his balance, he had to **use all his strength to** hold the line.
3. Although he didn't win the match, his **persistence** won everyone's **respect**.

**采分点**：① struggle with + only to；② to maintain 目的状语；③ although 让步 + win respect。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句 only to 后接动词原形（brought 错）；第 3 句用 win 的过去式 won。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为定语从句：The old man, who struggled with the shark for hours, brought back only a skeleton.
