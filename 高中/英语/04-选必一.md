## E-4.1 Feeling good

*选必一 Unit 1 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：幸福与情绪 ｜ 语法：过去完成时 ｜ 本单元 63 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："When I arrived, the film had started." 中 had started 和 arrived 谁先发生？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"幸福感"：读一篇关于快乐的科普文章（Is chocolate the answer?），学**过去完成时**，写一封**讲述个人经历的信**。词汇集中在情绪、健康、饮食与科学研究。

### 30 秒自评

1. 过去完成时表示"过去的过去"吗？
2. "by the time + 过去时"的主句常用什么时态？
3. 过去完成时能否与具体过去时间点连用？

> **答案**：1. 是　2. 过去完成时（had done）　3. 一般不，除非强调"到那时为止"

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **associate** v. 联想；联系：**associate** A with B 把 A 与 B 联系起来；be **associated** with 与……有关
- **essential** adj. 必不可少的：be **essential** to 对……至关重要；**essentially** adv. 本质上
- **vital** adj. 至关重要的：be **vital** to 对……至关重要；**vitality** n. 活力
- **anxiety** n. 焦虑：**anxiety** about 对……的焦虑；**anxious** adj. 焦虑的
- **optimistic** adj. 乐观的：be **optimistic** about 对……乐观；**optimism** n. 乐观
- **release** v./n. 释放：**release** pressure 释放压力；**release** a film 发行电影
- **mood** n. 情绪：in a good **mood** 心情好；**mood** swings 情绪波动
- **affect** v. 影响：**affect** mood 影响情绪；be **affected** by 受……影响
- **appreciate** v. 欣赏；感激：**appreciate** the beauty 欣赏美；**appreciate** your help 感激你的帮助
- **commitment** n. 投入；承诺：make a **commitment** 作出承诺；**commit** v. 投入

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be associated with | 与……有关 | Happiness **is associated with** good sleep. |
| be essential to | 对……必不可少 | Friends are **essential to** mental health. |
| suffer from anxiety | 受焦虑困扰 | Many students **suffer from anxiety**. |
| release pressure | 释放压力 | Exercise helps **release pressure**. |
| in a good mood | 心情好 | She is **in a good mood** today. |
| appreciate the importance of | 认识到……的重要性 | We should **appreciate the importance of** rest. |
| make a commitment | 作出承诺 | He **made a commitment** to exercise daily. |
| by the time | 到……的时候 | **By the time** we arrived, the show **had** ended. |

### 📐 语法：过去完成时

**结构**：had + 过去分词

| 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| 过去的过去 | When I arrived, the film **had started**. |
| 到过去某时为止 | By last year, he **had written** three books. |
| 与 before/after 连用 | She **had left** before I called. |
| 虚拟语气（if 从句） | If I **had known**, I would have come. |

**要点**
1. 与一般过去时搭配：先发生的用 had done，后发生的用 did
2. 常见标志：by the time, before, after, already, never, ever
3. 宾语从句中，主句过去时，从句动作更早用 had done

**易错边界**
1. 两个动作顺序颠倒：When I arrived, the film started.（若想表达"已开始"，用 had started）
2. 与具体过去时间连用：I had seen him yesterday.（×，应 saw）
3. 现在完成与过去完成混用：By now, I had finished.（×，应为 have finished）

**三个练习**
1. By the time we got to the cinema, the film ________ (begin). → **had begun**
2. She ________ (not finish) her homework before dinner. → **hadn't finished**
3. 改错：After he had eaten, he goes out. → **went out**

### ✍️ 写作：讲述个人经历的信

**结构**：称呼 → 写信目的 → 经历（时间线：先发生用 had done）→ 感受与收获 → 期待回信

**五个可套用句式**
1. **I'm writing to tell you about...** 我写信是想告诉你……
2. **It all started when...** 一切始于……
3. **By the time I..., ... had already...** 等我……时，……已经……
4. **What I learned from it was that...** 我从中领悟到……
5. **I hope you can share your story too.** 希望你也分享你的故事。

### 📖 阅读技能：把握作者语气

科普文章常先提出问题，再给研究，最后给建议。注意 **however, surprisingly, in fact** 后的转折与强调，那通常是作者真正的观点。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：essential / anxiety / optimistic / release / appreciate

1. Sleep is ______ to good health.
2. She felt ______ before the exam.
3. He is ______ about the future.
4. Music can help ______ pressure.
5. I really ______ your help.

**解析**：1. essential　2. anxious（名词 anxiety 此处需形容词）　3. optimistic　4. release　5. appreciate

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题 feel 后接形容词 anxious，不能填名词 anxiety。

**变式**：用 be associated with 造句，表达"规律锻炼与好心情有关"。→ Regular exercise is associated with a good mood.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用括号中动词的适当形式填空：
1. By the time we arrived, the concert ________ (start).
2. She told me she ________ (never see) the sea before.
3. After he ________ (finish) the report, he went home.

**解析**：1. had started　2. had never seen　3. had finished

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题直接引语变间接引语时，现在完成要变为过去完成。

**变式**：把 "I had finished the book before the film came out." 改为一般过去时（改变原意）。→ I finished the book before the film came out.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 等我到达车站时，火车已经开走了。
2. 研究表明，睡眠不足会影响情绪，而运动能释放压力。
3. 他承认，在参加志愿活动之前，他从未真正体会过幸福的含义。

**参考译文**：
1. **By the time** I got to the station, the train **had** already **left**.
2. Research shows that **lack of sleep affects mood**, while exercise can **release pressure**.
3. He admitted that he **had never really appreciated** the meaning of happiness before he **took part in** the volunteer work.

**采分点**：① by the time + had left；② show that + affect + while；③ admitted that + 过去完成 + before 从句。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句用 left（未体现"已"）；第 3 句 before 从句用过去完成（应为一般过去）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为 "Hardly had...when..." 倒装句：Hardly had I got to the station when the train left.

## E-4.2 Making a difference

*选必一 Unit 2 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：改变与公益 ｜ 语法：将来进行时 ｜ 本单元 67 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："This time tomorrow I will fly to London." 和 "will be flying" 有什么区别？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讲"带来改变"：读一个海岛社区的故事（Island story），学**将来进行时**，写一篇**支持慈善机构的观点文章**。词汇集中在环境保护、社区行动与传统文化。

### 30 秒自评

1. 将来进行时表示"将来某时正在进行"吗？
2. "will be doing" 可以让语气更委婉吗？
3. 将来进行时与一般将来时能互换吗？

> **答案**：1. 是　2. 可以（Will you be joining us? 更客气）　3. 多数情况可换，但强调"正在进行"时不能

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **preserve** v. 保护；保存：**preserve** traditions 保护传统；**preservation** n. 保护
- **ancestor** n. 祖先：our **ancestors** 我们的祖先；**ancestral** adj. 祖先的
- **shelter** n./v. 庇护所；遮蔽：take **shelter** 躲避；**shelter** from the rain 躲雨
- **advocate** v./n. 提倡；倡导者：**advocate** doing 提倡做；an **advocate** of ……的倡导者
- **conserve** v. 保护；节约：**conserve** energy 节约能源；**conservation** n. 保护
- **sponsor** v./n. 赞助；赞助者：**sponsor** a race 赞助比赛；a **sponsor** of ……的赞助者
- **transform** v. 转变：**transform** A into B 把 A 变成 B；**transformation** n. 转变
- **involve** v. 涉及；使参与：**involve** the community 让社区参与；be **involved** in 参与
- **perspective** n. 视角：from a different **perspective** 从不同角度；**perspective** on 对……的看法
- **distant** adj. 遥远的：a **distant** island 遥远的岛；**distance** n. 距离

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| make a difference | 有影响；起作用 | Small actions can **make a difference**. |
| preserve the tradition | 保护传统 | The festival helps **preserve the tradition**. |
| take shelter | 躲避 | We **took shelter** from the storm. |
| advocate doing | 提倡做 | They **advocate recycling** more. |
| conserve energy | 节约能源 | New buildings **conserve energy**. |
| sponsor a campaign | 赞助活动 | A local company **sponsored the campaign**. |
| transform... into... | 把……变成 | They **transformed** the old school **into** a museum. |
| from a global perspective | 从全球视角 | **From a global perspective**, the problem is urgent. |

### 📐 语法：将来进行时

**结构**：will be + 现在分词

| 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| 将来某时正在进行 | This time tomorrow I **will be flying** to London. |
| 按计划将发生 | We **will be meeting** at nine. |
| 委婉询问 | **Will you be joining** us for dinner? |
| 与一般将来时对比 | I **will talk** to him.（意愿）/ I **will be talking** to him at 3.（进行） |

**要点**
1. 强调"将来某一时刻正在做"，常与 this time tomorrow, at 8 tomorrow 连用
2. 也可表示"料想中必然发生"：Don't call at seven—we **will be having** dinner.
3. 一般不用于"临时决定"

**易错边界**
1. 与一般将来时混用：This time tomorrow I will fly.（语法可，但少了"正在进行"意味）
2. 状态动词用进行时：I will be knowing the answer.（×）
3. will be doing 与 be going to do 混用导致计划性不明

**三个练习**
1. This time next week, we ________ (sit) on the beach. → **will be sitting**
2. Don't phone at eight; I ________ (have) a meeting. → **will be having**
3. 改错：Will you be come to the party? → **Will you be coming to the party?**

### ✍️ 写作：支持慈善机构的观点文章

**结构**：引出机构与主张 → 3 个理由（事实/例子）→ 回应反对意见 → 呼吁行动

**五个可套用句式**
1. **I am writing to argue that...** 我写此文是想主张……
2. **The charity has been working to...** 该慈善机构一直致力于……
3. **For one thing, ...; for another, ...** 一方面……；另一方面……
4. **Some may argue that..., but...** 有人可能认为……，但是……

### 📖 阅读技能：识别"问题—解决"结构

环保/公益类文章常按"问题（现象+数据）→ 原因 → 解决方案 → 呼吁"展开。读时给每段贴标签（问题/原因/对策），主旨题就不容易选错。

2. Our ______ lived in this valley for centuries.

5. The project ______ the empty factory into a community centre.

**解析**：1. preserve　2. ancestors　3. shelter　4. advocate　5. transformed

**💥 陷阱**：第 4 题 advocate 后接动名词（advocate reducing），不接 to do。

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

1. This time tomorrow, I ________ (take) the exam.
2. Don't call at 7; we ________ (have) dinner.
3. ________ you ________ (join) us for the meeting at ten?

**解析**：1. will be taking　2. will be having　3. Will；be joining

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题疑问句语序：Will you be joining（will 提前，be 保留）。

**变式**：把 "I will be meeting him at three." 改为一般将来时并说明差别。→ I will meet him at three.（表示安排/意愿，不强调进行）

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 这个慈善机构一直致力于保护海岛的传统，并让当地年轻人参与其中。
2. 明天上午十点，我们将在社区中心讨论如何节约能源。
3. 从全球视角看，小小的改变也能带来巨大的影响。

**参考译文**：
1. The **charity** has been working to **preserve** the island's **traditions** and to **involve** local young people.
2. At ten tomorrow morning, we **will be discussing** how to **conserve energy** at the community centre.
3. **From a global perspective**, small changes can **make a big difference**.

**采分点**：① has been working to + involve；② 将来进行时 + how to do；③ from a global perspective。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句 involve 后漏 in（involve sb in）；第 2 句用 will discuss（未体现"正在"）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为被动语态：The island's traditions are being preserved by the charity, and local young people are being involved.

## E-4.3 Paying the price

*选必一 Unit 3 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：消费与广告 ｜ 语法：过去进行时与过去完成时的被动语态 ｜ 本单元 66 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："When I got home, the package had been delivered." 用了什么时态和语态？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"网红与消费"：读一篇关于网红营销的文章（The influencers），学**过去进行时与过去完成时的被动语态**，写一封**投诉信（letter of complaint）**。词汇集中在广告、品牌、合同与消费权益。

### 30 秒自评

1. 过去进行时的被动结构是 was/were being done 吗？
2. 过去完成时的被动结构是 had been done 吗？
3. 投诉信的语气应该强硬还是礼貌而坚定？

> **答案**：1. 是　2. 是　3. 礼貌而坚定（firm but polite）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **advertisement** n. 广告：put an **advertisement** 登广告；a misleading **advertisement** 误导性广告
- **celebrity** n. 名人：a **celebrity** endorsement 名人代言；**celebrities** 名人（复数）
- **genuine** adj. 真正的；真诚的：a **genuine** product 正品；**genuine** interest 真正的兴趣
- **contrary** adj./n. 相反的：**contrary** to 与……相反；on the **contrary** 恰恰相反
- **launch** v./n. 推出；发起：**launch** a product 推出产品；**launch** a campaign 发起活动
- **contract** n./v. 合同；收缩：sign a **contract** 签合同；**contract** a disease 染病
- **luxury** n. 奢侈品；奢华：a **luxury** brand 奢侈品牌；live in **luxury** 过奢华生活
- **purchase** v./n. 购买：**purchase** online 网购；make a **purchase** 购买
- **ethical** adj. 道德的：**ethical** standards 道德标准；**ethics** n. 伦理
- **complain** v. 投诉：**complain** about 投诉；**complaint** n. 投诉

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be contrary to | 与……相反 | The ad is **contrary to** the facts. |
| launch a product | 推出产品 | The company **launched** a new **product**. |
| sign a contract | 签合同 | Read the **contract** before you **sign** it. |
| make a purchase | 购买 | Keep the receipt after **making a purchase**. |
| complain about | 投诉 | She **complained about** the quality. |
| be aware of | 意识到 | Consumers should **be aware of** hidden costs. |
| be entitled to | 有权享有 | You **are entitled to** a refund. |
| in response to | 回应 | **In response to** your letter, we offer a refund. |

### 📐 语法：过去进行时与过去完成时的被动语态

| 时态 | 主动 | 被动 |
|---|---|---|
| 过去进行 | They **were repairing** the road. | The road **was being repaired**. |
| 过去完成 | They **had repaired** the road. | The road **had been repaired**. |

**用法**
1. 过去进行时被动：过去某时正在被……（was/were being done）
2. 过去完成时被动：到过去某时为止已经被……（had been done）
3. 常用于叙述中：When I arrived, the goods **were being loaded** and the bill **had been paid**.

**易错边界**
1. 漏 being：The road was repaired when I passed.（若强调"正在修"，应为 was being repaired）
2. had been 与 has been 混用：By last week, the order has been sent.（×）
3. 被动语态中的 by 短语滥用：The letter was written by him.（可，但不必每句都加）

**三个练习**
1. When I got there, the interview ________ (conduct) in the hall. → **was being conducted**
2. By the end of last month, all the tickets ________ (sell) out. → **had been sold**
3. 改错：The house was painted when we arrived. → **was being painted**（强调正在进行）

### ✍️ 写作：投诉信（letter of complaint）

**结构**：称呼 → 说明购买/服务情况 → 问题（时间、细节）→ 期望的解决方式 → 礼貌结尾

**五个可套用句式**
1. **I am writing to complain about...** 我写信投诉……
2. **I purchased... on... from your store.** 我于……在贵店购买了……
3. **To my disappointment, ...** 令我失望的是……
4. **I would like a refund / a replacement.** 我希望退款/换货。
5. **I look forward to your reply and a satisfactory solution.** 期待回复与满意的解决方案。

### 📖 阅读技能：识别广告中的说服手法

常见手法：**名人代言**（celebrity endorsement）、**从众**（everyone is buying it）、**稀缺**（only today）、**科学包装**（studies show）。读广告时问三个问题：谁在说？证据是什么？它在激发什么情绪？

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：celebrity / genuine / launch / contract / complain

1. The company will ______ a new phone next month.
2. Is this a ______ product or a fake?
3. She wrote to ______ about the poor service.
4. Read the ______ carefully before signing.
5. The ______ endorsed the brand for a huge fee.

**解析**：1. launch　2. genuine　3. complain　4. contract　5. celebrity

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题 complain 后接 about，不能直接接宾语。

**变式**：用 be contrary to 造句，表达"这则广告与事实相反"。→ The advertisement is contrary to the facts.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用被动语态改写：
1. They were interviewing the candidates when I arrived.
2. They had delivered the parcel before noon.
3. Someone was repairing my laptop at ten.

**解析**：
1. The candidates **were being interviewed** when I arrived.
2. The parcel **had been delivered** before noon.
3. My laptop **was being repaired** at ten.

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题用 were interviewed（一般过去被动），丢失"正在进行"。

**变式**：把 "By the time we got there, they had sold all the tickets." 改为被动。→ By the time we got there, all the tickets had been sold.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 我到达时，包裹正在被卸下，而账单已经被支付了。
2. 这则广告与事实相反，许多消费者因此投诉。
3. 我希望贵店能退款，并在今后遵守道德标准。

**参考译文**：
1. When I arrived, the parcels **were being unloaded** and the bill **had been paid**.
2. The **advertisement** is **contrary to** the facts, and many **consumers** have **complained** about it.
3. I hope your store will **give me a refund** and follow **ethical** standards in future.

**采分点**：① 过去进行被动 + 过去完成被动；② be contrary to + complain about；③ refund + ethical standards。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句两个被动时态混用；第 2 句 complain 后漏 about。

**变式**：把第 2 句改为非限制性定语从句：The advertisement, which is contrary to the facts, has led to many complaints.

> 答案：① was/were being done　② had been done　③ 礼貌而坚定

## E-4.4 The senses

*选必一 Unit 4 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频中 ｜ 话题：感官与味觉 ｜ 语法：表语从句 ｜ 本单元 67 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："The reason why he left is because he was tired." 这句话对吗？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讲"感官"：读一篇关于味觉的文章（A question of taste），学**表语从句**，写一份**调查报（survey report）**。词汇集中在味道、气味、感知与身体反应。

### 30 秒自评

1. 表语从句可以用 that, whether, what 引导吗？
2. "The reason is because..." 是规范表达吗？
3. 表语从句用陈述语序吗？

> **答案**：1. 可以　2. 不规范，常用 The reason is that...　3. 是

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **flavour** n. 味道：a strong **flavour** 浓烈的味道；**flavour** the soup 给汤调味
- **scent** n. 气味；香味：the **scent** of flowers 花香；**scented** adj. 有香味的
- **detect** v. 察觉：**detect** a change 察觉变化；**detection** n. 侦查
- **distinguish** v. 区分：**distinguish** A from B 区分 A 与 B；**distinguishable** adj. 可区分的
- **sensitive** adj. 敏感的：be **sensitive** to 对……敏感；**sensitivity** n. 敏感性
- **perceive** v. 感知：**perceive** colours 感知颜色；**perception** n. 感知
- **appetite** n. 食欲：lose one's **appetite** 没胃口；an **appetite** for 对……的渴望
- **claim** v./n. 声称：**claim** that 声称；make a **claim** 提出主张
- **combination** n. 组合：a **combination** of  ……的组合；**combine** v. 结合
- **react** v. 反应：**react** to 对……作出反应；**reaction** n. 反应

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| a combination of | ……的组合 | Taste is **a combination of** smell and texture. |
| be sensitive to | 对……敏感 | Some people **are sensitive to** bitter flavours. |
| distinguish A from B | 区分 A 与 B | Can you **distinguish** salt **from** sugar? |
| react to | 对……反应 | The tongue **reacts to** different chemicals. |
| lose one's appetite | 没胃口 | He **lost his appetite** after the long trip. |
| claim that | 声称 | The ad **claims that** the drink is healthy. |
| perceive... as... | 把……视为 | We **perceive** sweetness **as** pleasant. |
| be made up of | 由……组成 | The dish **is made up of** five ingredients. |

### 📐 语法：表语从句

**结构**：主语 + 系动词（be, seem, look, sound, taste, feel）+ 从句

| 引导词 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| that | The truth is **that** taste is personal. |
| whether | The question is **whether** we can trust our senses. |
| what | What matters is **what** you remember. |
| who / why / how | The reason is **why** the test failed. |
| because | That is **because** smell affects taste. |
| as if | It sounds **as if** the experiment worked. |

**要点**
1. 表语从句用**陈述语序**：The question is whether he **can** come.（不是 can he）
2. that 在表语从句中通常不省略
3. The reason is that...（不是 because）；That is because...（正确）

**易错边界**
1. 语序错误：The question is whether can he come.（×）
2. The reason is because...（×，规范用 that）
3. 表语从句与同位语从句混淆：The news that he won...（同位语）vs The fact is that he won...（表语）

**三个练习**
1. The problem is ________ we don't have enough data. → **that**
2. That is ________ I want to say. → **what**
3. 改错：The reason is because the soup is too salty. → **The reason is that the soup is too salty.**

### ✍️ 写作：调查报（survey report）

**结构**：调查目的与对象 → 主要发现（2–3 条，带数据）→ 原因分析 → 建议

**五个可套用句式**
1. **A survey was carried out among...** 在……中开展了调查。
2. **According to the survey, ...** 根据调查，……
3. **The majority of the students...** 大多数学生……
4. **This may be because...** 这可能是因为……
5. **It is suggested that...** 建议……

### 📖 阅读技能：感官描写

感官类文章常用**五感词**（sight, sound, smell, taste, touch）和比喻。读时把描写词分类，能快速抓住作者的态度（pleasant / unpleasant）；做词义猜测题时，上下文中的感官词就是线索。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：flavour / scent / detect / distinguish / sensitive

1. The ______ of coffee filled the room.
2. This ice cream has a strange ______.
3. Dogs can ______ smells that humans cannot.
4. It is hard to ______ between the twins.
5. Her skin is very ______ to the sun.

**解析**：1. scent　2. flavour　3. detect　4. distinguish　5. sensitive

**💥 陷阱**：第 4 题 distinguish 后接 between 或 A from B。

**变式**：用 a combination of 造句，表达"味道是气味与口感的组合"。→ Flavour is a combination of smell and texture.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用适当的引导词填空：
1. The question is ______ we should trust our senses.
2. That is ______ the experiment failed.
3. ______ surprised me was his sharp sense of smell.

**解析**：1. whether　2. why　3. What

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题用 if（表语从句一般用 whether）；第 2 题用 because（规范用 why/that）。

**变式**：把 "The reason is that the soup is too salty." 改为 "That is..." 句型。→ That is why the soup tastes too salty.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：
1. 研究表明，味觉是气味、口感与记忆的组合。
2. 问题在于我们能否相信自己的感官。
3. 大多数学生认为食堂的菜太咸，建议减少盐的用量。

**参考译文**：
1. Research shows that taste is **a combination of** smell, texture and memory.
2. The question is **whether** we can trust our own senses.
3. **The majority of** students think the food in the canteen is too salty and **suggest** reducing the amount of salt.

**采分点**：① a combination of；② whether 引导表语从句（陈述语序）；③ the majority of + suggest doing。

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 句语序错误（can we）；第 3 句 suggest 后接 to do（应为 doing）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为强调句：It is smell, texture and memory that make up taste.

> 答案：① 陈述语序　② that　③ that, whether, what, who, why, how, because, as if
