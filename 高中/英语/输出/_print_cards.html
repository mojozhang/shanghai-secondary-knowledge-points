E-1.1　Our world

⚡ 先猜："I'm very exciting about the trip." 这句话对吗？

30 秒概述

本单元围绕"我们生活的世界"展开：读一篇普通人一天的生活（Life in a day），学现在进行时的被动语态（am/is/are being done），写一封介绍新学校生活的非正式信件。核心词集中在日常作息、数字生活与个人目标上。

30 秒自评

  1. digital 的意思是"数字的"还是"手指的"？
  2. "The classroom is being cleaned." 用的是什么语态？
  3. "on schedule" 的意思是"按时"还是"在日程表上"？

答案：1. 数字的（finger 才是"手指"）　2. 现在进行时的被动语态　3. 按时

🔑 核心词汇（10）

  • digital adj. 数字的：a digital time capsule 数字时间胶囊；We live in a digital world.
  • typical adj. 典型的：a typical day 典型的一天；It is typical of him to be late.
  • routine n. 常规；惯例：daily routine 日常安排；My routine changes on Fridays.
  • scene n. 场面；现场：a familiar scene 熟悉的场景；the scene of the accident 事故现场
  • earn v. 挣得；赢得：earn a living 谋生；earn respect 赢得尊重
  • goal n. 目标；进球：set a goal 设定目标；score a goal 进一球
  • partner n. 搭档；伙伴：work with a partner 和搭档合作；a business partner 生意伙伴
  • improve v. 改进；提高：improve your English 提高英语；improve on the design 改进设计
  • challenge n./v. 挑战；质疑：face a challenge 面对挑战；challenge the result 质疑结果
  • inspire v. 激励；启发：inspire somebody to do 激励某人做；an inspiring story 鼓舞人心的故事

🧩 短语与搭配（8）

短语 意思 例句
on schedule 按时 The project finished on schedule.
be related to 与……有关 The two events are closely related to each other.
respond to 回应 She responded to my email at once.
a sense of freedom 自由感 Living alone gave him a sense of freedom.
at midnight 在午夜 The clock struck at midnight.
earn a living 谋生 He earns a living as a photographer.
improve on 对……加以改进 Can you improve on this plan?
inspire somebody to do 激励某人做 The talk inspired us to start a club.

📐 语法：现在进行时的被动语态

结构：am / is / are being + 过去分词

主语 形式 例句
I am being + done I am being watched.
he / she / it is being + done The road is being repaired.
we / they are being + done The trees are being planted.

用法：表示"此刻正在被……"，强调动作正在进行且主语是承受者。

与一般现在时被动的区别： - The classroom is cleaned every day.（每天都被打扫——习惯） - The classroom is being cleaned now.（此刻正在被打扫——进行）

  1. 漏掉 being：The road is repaired now.（×，缺"正在"）
  2. being 后误用动词原形：is being repair（×）

  3. 状态动词一般不用进行时：is being belonged to（×）

  4. The bridge __ (build) at the moment. → is being built

  5. Look! The car __ (wash) by my father. → is being washed
  6. 改错：The house is being paint by workers. → painted

✍️ 写作：非正式信件（informal letter）

结构：称呼 → 开头问候 → 主体（2 段：学校生活 + 感受）→ 结尾期待回信 → 署名

  1. How's it going? 最近怎么样？
  2. I've been here for a week, and I'm slowly getting used to... 我来这儿一周了，正慢慢适应……
  3. The thing I like most is... 我最喜欢的是……
  4. It's a bit hard to..., but I'm working on it. ……有点难，但我正在努力。
  5. Write back soon and tell me about... 快回信，跟我说说……

📖 阅读技能：找主题句

说明文/记叙文的主题句常在段首或段尾。先看首句，再看末句；如果首句是细节，末句往往总结。练习时用笔划出每段第一句，读完再回看是否覆盖全段。

例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：digital / typical / routine / earn / challenge

  1. It is a ______ day for a student: classes, lunch, homework.
  2. Many people ______ a living by writing.
  3. Learning a new language is a real ______.
  4. My morning ______ starts with a run.
  5. We live in a ______ age.

解析：1. typical　2. earn　3. challenge　4. routine　5. digital

💥 陷阱：第 1 题容易填 digital（"数字的一天"），但句意是"典型的一天"。

变式：用 inspire 造句，表达"这本书激励我学英语"。（The book inspired me to learn English.）

例题 2 · L2 语法

把下列句子改为现在进行时的被动语态： 1. They are building a new library. 2. Someone is repairing my bike. 3. The workers are planting trees.

解析： 1. A new library is being built. 2. My bike is being repaired. 3. Trees are being planted by the workers.

💥 陷阱：第 1 题漏掉 being，写成 is built，变成"一般现在时被动"。

变式：把 "The room is being cleaned." 改为一般疑问句。（Is the room being cleaned?）

例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子： 1. 这所学校正在被重建，所以我们暂时在临时教室里上课。 2. 让我印象最深的是这里的人：他们总是乐于回应别人的求助。 3. 虽然日程很紧，我还是设法按时完成了项目。

参考译文： 1. The school is being rebuilt, so we are having classes in temporary classrooms for now. 2. What impressed me most was the people here: they are always ready to respond to others' requests for help. 3. Although the schedule was tight, I managed to finish the project on schedule.

采分点：① 现在进行时被动 is being rebuilt；② impress 的用法 / respond to；③ on schedule 与 although 引导的让步状语从句。

💥 陷阱：第 1 句写成 is rebuilt（缺"正在"）；第 3 句把 on schedule 写成 in schedule。

变式：把第 2 句改为强调句：It was the people here that impressed me most.

E-1.2　Places

⚡ 先猜："I would visit the museum the next day." 里的 would 表示"过去将来"还是"客气"？

30 秒概述

本单元以"地方"为主题，读一篇介绍历史名城的文章（Where history comes alive），学过去将来时（would do / was going to do / was about to do），写一篇旅行博客。词汇集中在旅行、历史遗迹与个人感受。

30 秒自评

  1. historic 和 historical 都能表示"历史的"吗？
  2. "He said he would come." 中的 would 表示什么？
  3. "be about to do" 的意思是"即将做"还是"打算做"？

答案：1. 能，但 historic 强调"有历史意义的"，historical 强调"与历史有关的"　2. 过去将来（站在过去看将来）　3. 即将做（通常不与具体时间状语连用）

🔑 核心词汇（10）

  • destination n. 目的地：reach the destination 到达目的地；a tourist destination 旅游胜地
  • historic adj. 有历史意义的：a historic site 历史遗址；a historic moment 历史性时刻
  • former adj. 以前的：the former capital 旧都；a former teacher 以前的老师
  • view n. 景色；观点：a wonderful view 美景；in my view 在我看来
  • memory n. 记忆：childhood memories 童年记忆；in memory of 为了纪念
  • contribute v. 贡献；促成：contribute to 有助于；contribute money 捐钱
  • opportunity n. 机会：a golden opportunity 绝佳机会；take the opportunity to do 趁机做
  • transport n./v. 交通；运输：public transport 公共交通；transport goods 运输货物
  • disappoint v. 使失望：be disappointed at 对……失望；a disappointing result 令人失望的结果
  • adventure n. 冒险：an adventure story 冒险故事；go on an adventure 去冒险

🧩 短语与搭配（8）

短语 意思 例句
be famous for 因……而闻名 The city is famous for its old bridges.
date back to 追溯到 The temple dates back to the Tang Dynasty.
in memory of 为了纪念 The statue was built in memory of the poet.
contribute to 有助于；促成 Tourism contributes to the local economy.
take the opportunity to do 趁机做 We took the opportunity to visit the old town.
be worth visiting 值得参观 The museum is worth visiting.
get around 四处走动 It is easy to get around the city by bike.
in the distance 在远处 We saw a tower in the distance.

📐 语法：过去将来时

结构：would + 动词原形；was / were going to + 动词原形；was / were about to + 动词原形

形式 含义 例句
would do 站在过去看将来 He said he would visit the site.
was/were going to do 过去打算做 We were going to leave at six.
was/were about to do 过去即将做 She was about to speak when the bell rang.

用法：常用于宾语从句（said, thought, knew 之后）或叙述过去某时之后将发生的事。

  1. 与一般将来时混用：He said he will come.（×，主句过去时，从句用 would）
  2. was about to 后加具体时间：was about to leave at six（×，about to 强调"马上"，不加具体时间）

  3. would 与 used to 混淆：would 只表"过去常做（带感情）"，used to 强调"现在不做了"

  4. He told me he __ (come) back the next week. → would come

  5. They __ (leave) when it began to rain. → were about to leave
  6. 改错：She said she is going to visit the museum. → was going to

✍️ 写作：旅行博客（travel blog）

结构：吸引人的标题 → 时间地点 → 2–3 个亮点（用过去时叙事）→ 实用建议 → 一句感受

  1. If you ever get the chance, don't miss... 有机会千万别错过……
  2. The moment I stepped into..., I knew... 我一走进……就知道……
  3. What impressed me most was... 最让我印象深刻的是……
  4. A word of advice: ... 一句建议：……
  5. I left with a memory I will keep for ever. 我带着一段永远珍藏的记忆离开。

📖 阅读技能：扫读找细节（scanning）

先看题干中的关键词（人名、地名、数字、时间），再回到原文快速"扫"这些词，不必逐句读。找到关键词后，读它前后各一句，答案通常就在附近。

例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：destination / former / view / opportunity / transport

  1. The ______ capital of the country is now a quiet town.
  2. Public ______ in this city is cheap and clean.
  3. From the top of the hill, the ______ is amazing.
  4. Don't miss the ______ to try local food.
  5. Our final ______ is a small village by the sea.

解析：1. former　2. transport　3. view　4. opportunity　5. destination

💥 陷阱：第 1 题容易填 historic，但"旧都"强调"以前的"，用 former。

变式：用 contribute to 造句，表达"旅游业促进了这座城市的发展"。→ Tourism contributes to the development of the city.

例题 2 · L2 语法

把下列句子改为过去将来时（主句用过去时）： 1. She says she will visit the old town. 2. They are going to take the train. 3. We are about to leave.

解析： 1. She said she would visit the old town. 2. They said they were going to take the train. 3. We were about to leave when he called.

💥 陷阱：第 2 题把 are going to 只改成 was going to，忘记主谓一致（they → were）。

变式：把 "He told me he would come back soon." 改为直接引语。→ He said to me, "I will come back soon."

例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子： 1. 这座桥可以追溯到明朝，每年吸引数百万游客。 2. 我们本来打算第二天去博物馆，但一场大雨使我们改变了计划。 3. 在我看来，旅行的意义不在于目的地，而在于沿途的发现。

参考译文： 1. The bridge dates back to the Ming Dynasty and attracts millions of visitors every year. 2. We were going to visit the museum the next day, but a heavy rain made us change our plan. 3. In my view, the meaning of travel lies not in the destination but in the discoveries along the way.

采分点：① date back to + millions of；② were going to + make somebody do；③ in my view + not...but...

💥 陷阱：第 1 句 date back to 用成被动（is dated back to）；第 3 句 not...but... 结构不完整。

变式：把第 1 句改为定语从句：The bridge, which dates back to the Ming Dynasty, attracts millions of visitors every year.
