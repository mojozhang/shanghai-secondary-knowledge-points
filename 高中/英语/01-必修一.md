## E-1.1 Our world

### 30 秒概述

本单元围绕"我们生活的世界"展开：读一篇普通人一天的生活（Life in a day），学**现在进行时的被动语态**（am/is/are being done），写一封介绍新学校生活的**非正式信件**。核心词集中在日常作息、数字生活与个人目标上。

### 30 秒自评

1. digital 的意思是"数字的"还是"手指的"？
2. "The classroom is being cleaned." 用的是什么语态？
3. "on schedule" 的意思是"按时"还是"在日程表上"？

> **答案**：1. 数字的（finger 才是"手指"）　2. 现在进行时的被动语态　3. 按时

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **digital** adj. 数字的：a **digital** time capsule 数字时间胶囊；We live in a **digital** world.
- **typical** adj. 典型的：a **typical** day 典型的一天；It is **typical** of him to be late.
- **routine** n. 常规；惯例：daily **routine** 日常安排；My **routine** changes on Fridays.
- **scene** n. 场面；现场：a familiar **scene** 熟悉的场景；the **scene** of the accident 事故现场
- **earn** v. 挣得；赢得：**earn** a living 谋生；**earn** respect 赢得尊重
- **goal** n. 目标；进球：set a **goal** 设定目标；score a **goal** 进一球
- **partner** n. 搭档；伙伴：work with a **partner** 和搭档合作；a business **partner** 生意伙伴
- **improve** v. 改进；提高：**improve** your English 提高英语；**improve** on the design 改进设计
- **challenge** n./v. 挑战；质疑：face a **challenge** 面对挑战；**challenge** the result 质疑结果
- **inspire** v. 激励；启发：**inspire** somebody to do 激励某人做；an **inspiring** story 鼓舞人心的故事

### 📐 语法：现在进行时的被动语态

**结构**：am / is / are **being** + 过去分词

| 主语 | 形式 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I | am being + done | I **am being** watched. |
| he / she / it | is being + done | The road **is being** repaired. |
| we / they | are being + done | The trees **are being** planted. |

**用法**：表示"此刻正在被……"，强调动作正在进行且主语是承受者。

**与一般现在时被动的区别**：

- The classroom **is cleaned** every day.（每天都被打扫——习惯）
- The classroom **is being cleaned** now.（此刻正在被打扫——进行）

**易错边界**

1. 漏掉 being：The road is repaired now.（×，缺"正在"）
2. being 后误用动词原形：is being repair（×）
3. 状态动词一般不用进行时：is being belonged to（×）

**三个练习**

1. The bridge ________ (build) at the moment. → **is being built**
2. Look! The car ________ (wash) by my father. → **is being washed**
3. 改错：The house is being paint by workers. → **painted**

### ✍️ 写作：非正式信件（informal letter）

**结构**：称呼 → 开头问候 → 主体（2 段：学校生活 + 感受）→ 结尾期待回信 → 署名

**五个可套用句式**

1. **How's it going?** 最近怎么样？
2. **I've been here for a week, and I'm slowly getting used to...** 我来这儿一周了，正慢慢适应……
3. **The thing I like most is...** 我最喜欢的是……
4. **It's a bit hard to..., but I'm working on it.** ……有点难，但我正在努力。
5. **Write back soon and tell me about...** 快回信，跟我说说……

### 📖 阅读技能：找主题句

说明文/记叙文的主题句常在**段首或段尾**。先看首句，再看末句；如果首句是细节，末句往往总结。练习时用笔划出每段第一句，读完再回看是否覆盖全段。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：digital / typical / routine / earn / challenge

1. It is a ______ day for a student: classes, lunch, homework.
2. Many people ______ a living by writing.
3. Learning a new language is a real ______.
4. My morning ______ starts with a run.
5. We live in a ______ age.

**解析**：1. typical　2. earn　3. challenge　4. routine　5. digital

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题容易填 digital（"数字的一天"），但句意是"典型的一天"。

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

把下列句子改为现在进行时的被动语态：

1. They are building a new library.
2. Someone is repairing my bike.
3. The workers are planting trees.

**解析**：

1. A new library **is being built**.
2. My bike **is being repaired**.
3. Trees **are being planted** by the workers.

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题漏掉 being，写成 is built，变成"一般现在时被动"。

**变式**：把 "The room is being cleaned." 改为一般疑问句。（Is the room being cleaned?）

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 这所学校正在被重建，所以我们暂时在临时教室里上课。
2. 让我印象最深的是这里的人：他们总是乐于回应别人的求助。
3. 虽然日程很紧，我还是设法按时完成了项目。

**参考译文**：

1. The school **is being rebuilt**, so we are having classes in temporary classrooms for now.
2. What **impressed** me most was the people here: they are always ready to **respond to** others' requests for help.
3. Although the **schedule** was tight, I managed to finish the project **on schedule**.

**采分点**：① 现在进行时被动 is being rebuilt；② impress 的用法 / respond to；③ on schedule 与 although 引导的让步状语从句。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句写成 is rebuilt（缺"正在"）；第 3 句把 on schedule 写成 in schedule。

**变式**：把第 2 句改为强调句：It was the people here that impressed me most.

## E-1.2 Places

*必修一 Unit 2 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：地方与历史 ｜ 语法：过去将来时 ｜ 本单元 48 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："I would visit the museum the next day." 里的 would 表示"过去将来"还是"客气"？

### 30 秒概述

本单元以"地方"为主题，读一篇介绍历史名城的文章（Where history comes alive），学**过去将来时**（would do / was going to do / was about to do），写一篇**旅行博客**。词汇集中在旅行、历史遗迹与个人感受。

### 30 秒自评

1. historic 和 historical 都能表示"历史的"吗？
2. "He said he would come." 中的 would 表示什么？
3. "be about to do" 的意思是"即将做"还是"打算做"？

> **答案**：1. 能，但 historic 强调"有历史意义的"，historical 强调"与历史有关的"　2. 过去将来（站在过去看将来）　3. 即将做（通常不与具体时间状语连用）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **destination** n. 目的地：reach the **destination** 到达目的地；a tourist **destination** 旅游胜地
- **historic** adj. 有历史意义的：a **historic** site 历史遗址；a **historic** moment 历史性时刻
- **former** adj. 以前的：the **former** capital 旧都；a **former** teacher 以前的老师
- **view** n. 景色；观点：a wonderful **view** 美景；in my **view** 在我看来
- **memory** n. 记忆：childhood **memories** 童年记忆；in **memory** of 为了纪念
- **contribute** v. 贡献；促成：**contribute** to 有助于；**contribute** money 捐钱
- **opportunity** n. 机会：a golden **opportunity** 绝佳机会；take the **opportunity** to do 趁机做
- **transport** n./v. 交通；运输：public **transport** 公共交通；**transport** goods 运输货物
- **disappoint** v. 使失望：be **disappointed** at 对……失望；a **disappointing** result 令人失望的结果
- **adventure** n. 冒险：an **adventure** story 冒险故事；go on an **adventure** 去冒险

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be famous for | 因……而闻名 | The city **is famous for** its old bridges. |
| date back to | 追溯到 | The temple **dates back to** the Tang Dynasty. |
| in memory of | 为了纪念 | The statue was built **in memory of** the poet. |
| contribute to | 有助于；促成 | Tourism **contributes to** the local economy. |
| take the opportunity to do | 趁机做 | We **took the opportunity to** visit the old town. |
| be worth visiting | 值得参观 | The museum **is worth visiting**. |
| get around | 四处走动 | It is easy to **get around** the city by bike. |
| in the distance | 在远处 | We saw a tower **in the distance**. |

### 📐 语法：过去将来时

**结构**：would + 动词原形；was / were going to + 动词原形；was / were about to + 动词原形

| 形式 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| would do | 站在过去看将来 | He said he **would visit** the site. |
| was/were going to do | 过去打算做 | We **were going to** leave at six. |
| was/were about to do | 过去即将做 | She **was about to** speak when the bell rang. |

**用法**：常用于宾语从句（said, thought, knew 之后）或叙述过去某时之后将发生的事。

**易错边界**

1. 与一般将来时混用：He said he will come.（×，主句过去时，从句用 would）
2. was about to 后加具体时间：was about to leave at six（×，about to 强调"马上"，不加具体时间）
3. would 与 used to 混淆：would 只表"过去常做（带感情）"，used to 强调"现在不做了"

**三个练习**

1. He told me he ________ (come) back the next week. → **would come**
2. They ________ (leave) when it began to rain. → **were about to leave**
3. 改错：She said she is going to visit the museum. → **was going to**

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

把下列句子改为过去将来时（主句用过去时）：

1. She says she will visit the old town.
2. They are going to take the train.
3. We are about to leave.

**解析**：

1. She said she **would visit** the old town.
2. They said they **were going to take** the train.
3. We **were about to leave** when he called.

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题把 are going to 只改成 was going to，忘记主谓一致（they → were）。

**变式**：把 "He told me he would come back soon." 改为直接引语。→ He said to me, "I will come back soon."

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 这座桥可以追溯到明朝，每年吸引数百万游客。
2. 我们本来打算第二天去博物馆，但一场大雨使我们改变了计划。
3. 在我看来，旅行的意义不在于目的地，而在于沿途的发现。

**参考译文**：

1. The bridge **dates back to** the Ming Dynasty and attracts **millions of** visitors every year.
2. We **were going to** visit the museum the next day, but a heavy rain made us change our plan.
3. **In my view**, the meaning of travel lies not in the **destination** but in the discoveries along the way.

**采分点**：① date back to + millions of；② were going to + make somebody do；③ in my view + not...but...

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句 date back to 用成被动（is dated back to）；第 3 句 not...but... 结构不完整。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为定语从句：The bridge, which dates back to the Ming Dynasty, attracts millions of visitors every year.

## E-1.3 Choices

*必修一 Unit 3 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：选择与消费 ｜ 语法：关系代词引导的定语从句 ｜ 本单元 47 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："The vending machine who sells snacks is popular." 这句话错在哪里？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"选择"：读一篇关于食物与消费的文章（The good, the bad and the really ugly），学**关系代词 who / whom / which / that / whose** 引导的定语从句，写**邀请、便条等简短信息**。词汇集中在包装、回收、消费与判断。

### 30 秒自评

1. which 可以用来指人吗？
2. whose 在定语从句中表示"所属关系"吗？
3. 关系代词在从句中作宾语时可以省略吗？

> **答案**：1. 不能（指人用 who/whom，that 可指人也可指物）　2. 是　3. 可以（作宾语且非正式时）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **convenience** n. 便利：for **convenience** 为了方便；a **convenience** store 便利店
- **label** n./v. 标签；贴标签：read the **label** 看标签；**label** the box 给盒子贴标签
- **recycle** v. 回收利用：**recycle** paper 回收纸；a **recycling** bin 回收箱
- **facility** n. 设施：public **facilities** 公共设施；sports **facilities** 体育设施
- **amount** n. 数量：a large **amount** of 大量的（接不可数名词）
- **impact** n. 影响：have an **impact** on 对……有影响
- **determine** v. 决定；确定：**determine** to do 决心做；be **determined** to do 下定决心做
- **source** n. 来源：the **source** of energy 能源来源；a reliable **source** 可靠来源
- **consumer** n. 消费者：**consumer** rights 消费者权益；a **consumer** society 消费社会
- **establish** v. 建立；确立：**establish** a company 创办公司；**establish** a habit 养成习惯

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| a large amount of | 大量的 | The factory uses **a large amount of** water. |
| have an impact on | 对……有影响 | Packaging **has an impact on** the environment. |
| be determined to do | 下定决心做 | She **was determined to** change her diet. |

### 📐 语法：关系代词引导的定语从句

| 关系代词 | 指代 | 在从句中作 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| who | 人 | 主语/宾语 | the girl **who** won the prize |
| whom | 人 | 宾语（正式） | the man **whom** we met |
| which | 物 | 主语/宾语 | the box **which** is made of plastic |
| that | 人/物 | 主语/宾语 | the book **that** I bought |
| whose | 人/物 | 定语（所属） | the student **whose** idea won |

**要点**

1. 关系代词作宾语时可以省略：the book (that) I bought
2. 介词提前时只能用 whom / which：the person **to whom** I spoke
3. **非限制性**定语从句用逗号隔开，不能用 that：My teacher, **who** is from Canada, ...

**易错边界**

1. 指人用 which：the man which helped me（×）
2. 从句缺主语却省略关系代词：the book I bought it（×，it 多余）
3. whose 后直接跟名词：whose **idea**（√），whose **is** idea（×）

**三个练习**

1. This is the shop ________ sells recycled bags. → **which / that**
2. The girl ________ hair is curly is my sister. → **whose**
3. 改错：The man who he called me is my uncle. → 去掉 he

### ✍️ 写作：简短信息（invitation / note）

**便条结构**：称呼 → 事由（1 句）→ 时间地点（1 句）→ 期待/提醒（1 句）→ 署名

**五个可套用句式**

1. **I'm writing to invite you to...** 我写信是想邀请你……
2. **It will be held at... on...** 活动将于……在……举行。
3. **Would you like to join us?** 你愿意加入我们吗？

4. For ______, the shop stays open until midnight.
5. Fast food has a big ______ on our health.

**解析**：1. label　2. recycle　3. facility　4. convenience　5. impact

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题 facility 常用复数 facilities，注意主谓一致（is open 单数，此处指一个场馆）。

**变式**：用 a large amount of 造句，表达"这家工厂用了大量塑料"。→ The factory uses a large amount of plastic.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

合并句子（用关系代词）：

1. I know a girl. Her father is a doctor.
2. This is the bag. I bought it yesterday.
3. The man is our teacher. We met him at the gate.

**解析**：

1. I know a girl **whose** father is a doctor.
2. This is the bag **(which/that)** I bought yesterday.
3. The man **(whom/who/that)** we met at the gate is our teacher.

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题保留 him，从句出现两个宾语；第 1 题用 who 代替 whose。

**变式**：把第 2 句改为非限制性定语从句。→ This bag, which I bought yesterday, is made of recycled plastic.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 这家便利店使用可回收包装，这对环境有积极影响。
2. 那个建议我们少买塑料瓶的女孩是我的同桌。
3. 尽管价格更高，消费者仍然愿意购买有环保标签的产品。

**参考译文**：

1. The **convenience** store uses **recyclable** packaging, **which has a positive impact on** the environment.
2. The girl **who advised us to buy fewer plastic bottles** is my deskmate.
3. **Despite** the higher price, **consumers** are still willing to buy products **with** an eco-friendly **label**.

**采分点**：① which 引导非限制性定语从句；② who 引导定语从句 + advise sb to do；③ despite + be willing to do。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句用 what 引导从句（×）；第 3 句 despite 后接句子（×，应接名词）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为两个简单句。→ The convenience store uses recyclable packaging. This has a positive impact on the environment.

> 答案：① 定语　② 不能　③ 不可数

## E-1.4 My space

*必修一 Unit 4 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：家与空间 ｜ 语法：关系副词引导的定语从句 ｜ 本单元 51 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："This is the house where I lived in last year." 这句话对吗？

### 30 秒概述

本单元以"我的空间"为主题，读一篇关于 1940 年代老房子的文章（The 1940s house），学**关系副词 when / where / why** 引导的定语从句，写一篇**描述房间**的文章。词汇集中在住房、家用设备与生活安排。

### 30 秒自评

1. where 在定语从句中相当于"介词 + which"吗？
2. why 引导定语从句时，先行词通常是 reason 吗？
3. "the day when we met" 中的 when 可以换成 that 吗？

> **答案**：1. 是（where = in/at/on which）　2. 是　3. 可以（the day that we met，that 作关系副词较少见但可接受；更常见省略）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **experiment** n./v. 实验：do an **experiment** 做实验；**experiment** with 尝试
- **capable** adj. 有能力的：be **capable** of doing 有能力做
- **survive** v. 幸存；挺过：**survive** the winter 熬过冬天；**survival** n. 生存
- **device** n. 装置：a labour-saving **device** 省力装置；electronic **devices** 电子设备
- **regularly** adv. 定期地：check **regularly** 定期检查；exercise **regularly** 经常锻炼
- **aim** n./v. 目标；旨在：**aim** to do 旨在做；achieve one's **aim** 达到目标
- **suffer** v. 遭受：**suffer** from 遭受（疾病、困难）；**suffering** n. 痛苦
- **decrease** v./n. 减少：**decrease** by 减少了；**decrease** to 减少到
- **recommend** v. 推荐：**recommend** doing 建议做；**recommend** somebody to do 建议某人做
- **responsibility** n. 责任：take **responsibility** for 对……负责；a sense of **responsibility** 责任感

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be capable of | 有能力做 | The device **is capable of** saving water. |
| survive on | 靠……生存 | Some plants **survive on** very little water. |
| aim to do | 旨在做 | The project **aims to** cut energy use. |
| suffer from | 遭受 | Many old houses **suffer from** damp. |
| decrease by / to | 减少了／到 | Sales **decreased by** 10% last year. |
| recommend doing | 建议做 | I **recommend** checking the roof **regularly**. |
| take responsibility for | 对……负责 | She **takes responsibility for** the repairs. |
| prefer A to B | 更喜欢 A | I **prefer** the small room **to** the big one. |

### 📐 语法：关系副词引导的定语从句

| 关系副词 | 先行词 | 相当于 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| when | 时间 | in/on/at which | the year **when** we moved here |
| where | 地点 | in/on/at which | the room **where** I study |
| why | 原因（reason） | for which | the reason **why** he left |

**要点**

1. 关系副词在从句中作**状语**，从句不缺主语或宾语
2. where 的先行词可以是抽象地点：a point **where** we disagree
3. the reason why 后从句结构完整；若从句缺主语/宾语，用 that/which

**易错边界**

1. where 后重复介词：the house where I lived **in**（×）
2. 先行词是地点却用 which 且从句缺状语：the room which I study（×，应为 where/in which）
3. when 与 that 混用导致从句缺状语：the day that we met **on**（×，去掉 on 或改用 when）

**三个练习**

1. This is the village ________ my grandparents live. → **where**
2. I still remember the day ________ we first met. → **when**
3. 改错：The reason why he was late because he missed the bus. → **that** he missed the bus（去掉 because）

1. **The first thing you notice is...** 你首先注意到的是……

### 📖 阅读技能：识别指代（reference）

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：experiment / capable / device / regularly / responsibility

1. The new ______ can save half of the electricity.
2. She is ______ of fixing the old piano.
3. We do an ______ every Friday afternoon.
4. He checks the data ______.
5. Taking care of the room is my ______.

**解析**：1. device　2. capable　3. experiment　4. regularly　5. responsibility

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题 capable 后必须接 of doing，不能直接接动词原形。

**变式**：用 aim to do 造句，表达"这个项目旨在减少能源消耗"。→ The project aims to reduce energy consumption.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用适当的关系副词填空：

1. This is the flat ______ we lived for ten years.
2. I'll never forget the morning ______ the letter arrived.
3. Nobody knows the reason ______ she left so suddenly.

**解析**：1. **where**　2. **when**　3. **why**

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题从句 live 后不需要介词（live 不及物，where = in which），不要写成 where... in。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为"介词 + which"结构。→ This is the flat in which we lived for ten years.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 这是我小时候住过的房间，窗边放着一架旧钢琴。
2. 他建议我们定期检查电器，以免发生事故。
3. 尽管房子很小，它却是一个能让我放松的地方。

**参考译文**：

1. This is the room **where** I lived as a child, **with** an old piano **by the window**.
2. He **recommends** checking the **devices regularly** to **prevent** accidents.
3. **Although** the house is small, it is a place **where** I can relax.

**采分点**：① where 引导定语从句 + with 复合结构；② recommend doing + regularly；③ although 让步 + where 定语从句。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句 where 后加 in；第 2 句 recommend 后接 to do（应为 doing 或 recommend sb to do）。

**变式**：把第 3 句改为"so...that"结构。→ The house is so small that it is easy to keep clean. / It is such a relaxing place that I often stay there.

> 答案：① 介词 + which　② why（或 that，视从句成分）　③ by 表幅度，to 表结果
