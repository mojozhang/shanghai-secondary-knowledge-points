## E-7.1 The media and the message

*选必四 Unit 1 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：新闻与真相 ｜ 语法：情态动词表可能性 ｜ 本单元 48 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："The story may be true." 和 "The story must be true." 哪个把握更大？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讲"媒体与信息"：读一篇关于新闻生产的文章（Now in the news），学**情态动词表可能性**，根据一条新闻标题写**观点文章**。词汇集中在新闻、证据、偏见与判断。

### 30 秒自评

1. must 表可能性时，把握最大吗？
2. can't 可以表示"不可能"吗？
3. may 与 might 的可能性程度一样吗？

> **答案**：1. 是（几乎肯定）　2. 可以　3. might 略低/更不确定

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **testimony** n. 证词；证明：give **testimony** 作证；**testify** v. 作证
- **footage** n. 视频片段：news **footage** 新闻画面；rare **footage** 罕见影像
- **compelling** adj. 有说服力的：**compelling** evidence 有力证据；a **compelling** story 引人入胜的故事
- **reliable** adj. 可靠的：a **reliable** source 可靠来源；**rely** v. 依赖
- **coverage** n. 报道；覆盖：news **coverage** 新闻报道；media **coverage** 媒体报道
- **witness** n./v. 目击者；目击：a **witness** to  ……的目击者；**witness** the event 目击事件
- **prejudice** n. 偏见：**prejudice** against 对……的偏见；**prejudiced** adj. 有偏见的
- **expectation** n. 期望：beyond **expectation** 出乎意料；meet one's **expectations** 达到期望
- **suspect** v./n. 怀疑；嫌疑人：**suspect** that 怀疑；a **suspect** 嫌疑人
- **instinct** n. 本能：by **instinct** 出于本能；**instinctive** adj. 本能的

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| according to | 根据 | **According to** the report, the story is true. |
| be based on | 基于 | The article **is based on** **testimony**. |
| check the source | 核实来源 | Always **check the source** of a story. |
| be accused of | 被指控 | The paper **was accused of** **prejudice**. |
| in the public interest | 符合公共利益 | The report was published **in the public interest**. |
| jump to conclusions | 匆忙下结论 | Don't **jump to conclusions**. |
| be aware of | 意识到 | Readers should **be aware of** bias. |
| take... seriously | 认真对待 | We should **take** the warning **seriously**. |

### 📐 语法：情态动词表可能性

| 情态动词 | 可能性 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| must | 几乎肯定 | The story **must be** true. |
| may / might / could | 可能 | It **may be** a mistake. |
| can't | 不可能 | That **can't be** the whole story. |
| should | 按理应该 | The report **should be** reliable. |

**要点**

1. must 表"肯定推测"，can't 表"否定推测"；may/might/could 表"可能"
2. 对过去的推测用 must/may/might/could + have done
3. 情态动词表可能性时不用于疑问句（must 除外，表"必须"）

**易错边界**

1. 用 mustn't 表推测：He mustn't be at home.（×，表禁止）
2. 可能性程度混用：may 与 must 等同（×）
3. 过去推测漏 have done：He must be ill yesterday.（×）

**三个练习**

1. The light is on. She ________ (be) at home. → **must be**
2. He ________ (not finish) so quickly; it's impossible. → **can't have finished**
3. 改错：It may be happened yesterday. → **It may have happened yesterday.**

### ✍️ 写作：根据新闻标题写观点文章

**结构**：引述标题与事件 → 我的立场 → 2 个理由（证据/例子）→ 回应反方 → 结论

**五个可套用句式**

1. **The headline "..." has raised heated discussion.** 标题"……"引发了热议。
2. **From my perspective, ...** 在我看来，……
3. **The evidence suggests that...** 证据表明……
4. **Some may argue that..., but...** 有人可能认为……，但是……
5. **It is important that we...** 重要的是我们……

### 📖 阅读技能：判断信息来源

新闻类文章先找**来源**（according to, a study by, witnesses say）、**时间**与**数字**。来源不明或只有单一来源时，结论要谨慎；题目问 "Which of the following is a fact?" 时，选可核实的信息。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：testimony / compelling / reliable / coverage / prejudice

1. The **witness**'s ______ was recorded by the police.
2. The documentary offers ______ evidence.
3. We need a ______ source of information.
4. The event received wide media ______.
5. We should fight against ______.

**解析**：1. testimony　2. compelling　3. reliable　4. coverage　5. prejudice

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题 testimony 不可数，不加 s。

**变式**：用 be aware of 造句，表达"读者应意识到新闻中的偏见"。→ Readers should be aware of prejudice in the news.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用情态动词填空（表可能性）：

1. The ground is wet. It ________ (rain) last night.
2. She ________ (be) at home now; the light is on.
3. He ________ (not finish) the work so fast; it's impossible.
4. The news ________ (be) true, but we need more evidence.

**解析**：1. must have rained　2. must be　3. can't have finished　4. may/might/could be

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题用 mustn't have finished（×）。

**变式**：把 "Maybe he is right." 改为情态动词句。→ He may/might be right.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 这段视频可能被剪辑过，我们不能只凭它下结论。
2. 根据目击者的证词，事故发生在午夜前后。
3. 媒体应当核实来源，避免传播带有偏见的信息。

**参考译文**：

1. The **footage** **may have been** edited; we can't **jump to conclusions** based on it alone.
2. **According to** the **witness**'s **testimony**, the accident happened around midnight.
3. The media should **check the source** and avoid spreading information full of **prejudice**.

**采分点**：① may have been done；② according to + testimony；③ check the source + avoid doing。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句用 may be edited（未体现"可能已经"）；第 3 句 avoid 后接 to spread（×）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为被动句：The footage may have been edited by someone.

> 答案：① 几乎肯定　② can't/couldn't　③ 名词/名词短语（来源）

## E-7.2 Live longer, live better?

*选必四 Unit 2 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频高 ｜ 话题：长寿与伦理 ｜ 语法：虚拟语气（非真实条件句） ｜ 本单元 53 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："If I were you, I would..." 中为什么用 were 而不是 was？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"长寿"：读一篇关于延长寿命的想象文（From here to eternity），学**虚拟语气（非真实条件句）**，写一篇关于老龄化的**说服性文章**。词汇集中在医学、风险、伦理与情感。

### 30 秒自评

1. 与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句，从句用过去时吗？
2. 与过去事实相反时，主句用 would have done 吗？
3. if 从句中有 were/had/should 时，可以省略 if 并倒装吗？

> **答案**：1. 是（if + did/were）　2. 是　3. 可以（Were I..., Had I..., Should you...）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **immortality** n. 永生：the dream of **immortality** 永生的梦想；**immortal** adj. 不朽的
- **transplant** n./v. 移植：a heart **transplant** 心脏移植；**transplant** organs 移植器官
- **pose** v. 造成；提出：**pose** a threat 构成威胁；**pose** a question 提出问题
- **severe** adj. 严重的；严厉的：**severe** pain 剧痛；**severely** adv. 严重地
- **resistance** n. 抵抗；阻力：**resistance** to change 对变革的抵制；**resist** v. 抵抗
- **sustain** v. 维持：**sustain** life 维持生命；**sustainable** adj. 可持续的
- **urgent** adj. 紧急的：an **urgent** need 迫切需要；**urgency** n. 紧迫
- **owe** v. 欠；归功于：**owe** somebody something 欠某人某物；**owe** ... to ... 把……归功于
- **tension** n. 紧张；张力：**tension** between  ……之间的紧张关系；**tense** adj. 紧张的
- **vulnerable** adj. 脆弱的：be **vulnerable** to 易受……伤害；**vulnerability** n. 脆弱

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| pose a threat to | 对……构成威胁 | Ageing may **pose a threat to** health systems. |
| be vulnerable to | 易受……影响 | Older people **are vulnerable to** infection. |
| sustain life | 维持生命 | Clean water **sustains life**. |
| be resistant to | 对……有抵抗力 | Some bacteria **are resistant to** drugs. |
| owe... to... | 把……归功于 | We **owe** our health **to** science. |
| in the face of | 面对 | **In the face of** ageing, we need new ideas. |
| relieve tension | 缓解紧张 | Music can **relieve tension**. |
| take urgent action | 采取紧急行动 | We must **take urgent action**. |

### 📐 语法：虚拟语气（非真实条件句）

| 时间 | 从句 | 主句 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 与现在相反 | did / were | would/could/might do | If I **were** rich, I **would travel**. |
| 与过去相反 | had done | would have done | If I **had known**, I **would have come**. |
| 与将来相反 | did / were to do / should do | would do | If it **should rain**, we **would stay**. |
| 混合 | had done | would do（现在） | If I **had taken** the job, I **would be** in Tokyo now. |

**省略 if 的倒装**：Were I you... / Had I known... / Should it rain...

**wish / if only**：I wish I **were** taller. / I wish I **had studied** harder.

**易错边界**

1. 从句用 would：If I would be rich...（×）
2. 过去虚拟主句漏 have：I would came（×）
3. were 与 was 在正式虚拟中：If I **was** you（口语可，书面用 were）

**三个练习**

1. If I ________ (be) you, I would accept the offer. → **were**
2. If she ________ (leave) earlier, she wouldn't have missed the train. → **had left**
3. 改错：If he would have known, he would have helped. → **If he had known**

### ✍️ 写作：说服性文章（persuasive essay）

**结构**：提出问题 → 我方立场 → 3 个论据（数据/例子/对比）→ 反驳对方 → 呼吁

**五个可套用句式**

1. **It is urgent that we...** 我们迫切需要……
2. **If we did nothing, ... would...** 如果我们什么都不做，……将会……
3. **The evidence is clear: ...** 证据很清楚：……
4. **Opponents may argue that..., yet...** 反对者可能认为……，然而……
5. **It is time we took action.** 我们该采取行动了。

### 📖 阅读技能：识别假设与事实

科普/伦理文章常用 **if, would, could, imagine** 引出假设，用 **in fact, actually, studies show** 引出事实。做题时先标记"假设句"，避免把假设当成作者主张。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：immortality / transplant / pose / severe / vulnerable

1. The idea of ______ has fascinated people for centuries.
2. He received a kidney ______ last year.
3. Climate change may ______ a threat to coastal cities.
4. She suffered ______ pain after the accident.
5. Young children are ______ to infection.

**解析**：1. immortality　2. transplant　3. pose　4. severe　5. vulnerable

**💥 陷阱**：第 5 题 vulnerable 后接 to。

**变式**：用 pose a threat to 造句，表达"空气污染对健康构成威胁"。→ Air pollution poses a threat to health.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用括号中动词的适当形式填空：

1. If I ________ (know) the answer, I would tell you.
2. If they ________ (start) earlier, they would have finished.
3. If it ________ (rain) tomorrow, the match would be put off.
4. I wish I ________ (not eat) so much last night.

**解析**：1. knew　2. had started　3. should rain / were to rain　4. hadn't eaten

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 题用 started（与过去相反需 had started）。

**变式**：把 "If I had known, I would have helped." 改为省略 if 的倒装句。→ Had I known, I would have helped.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 如果我们不采取紧急行动，老龄化将给医疗系统带来巨大压力。
2. 要是没有器官移植技术，许多病人不可能活到今天。
3. 与其追求永生，不如让每个年龄段的人都活得有尊严。

**参考译文**：

1. If we **did not take urgent action**, ageing **would pose** enormous pressure on health systems.
2. **Without** organ **transplant** technology, many patients **could not have survived**.
3. **Rather than** pursue **immortality**, we should help people of every age live with **dignity**.

**采分点**：① 虚拟条件句；② without + 名词 + could have done；③ rather than + 动词原形。

**💥 陷阱**：第 2 句用 could not survive（未体现"活到今天"）；第 3 句 rather than 后接 to pursue（×）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为 "Were we not to take urgent action..." 倒装句。

> 答案：① if + did/were, would do　② would have done　③ Were..., Had..., Should...

## E-7.3 Food and ethics

*选必四 Unit 3 ｜ Ⅱ · 考频中 ｜ 话题：食物与伦理 ｜ 语法：被动语态的各种形式 ｜ 本单元 65 词*

**⚡ 先猜**："It is said that..." 中的 it 指代什么？

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"吃什么、怎么吃"：读一篇关于食物伦理的文章（Would you eat it?），系统复习**被动语态的各种形式**，写一篇关于**素食的正反观点文章**。词汇集中在物种、环境、食品与伦理。

### 30 秒自评

1. "It is said that..." 中 it 是形式主语吗？
2. 不定式的被动形式是 to be done 吗？
3. 被动语态可以用于完成进行时吗？

> **答案**：1. 是　2. 是　3. 很少用（have been being done 不自然，通常避免）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **species** n. 物种：endangered **species** 濒危物种；a **species** of 一种
- **prohibit** v. 禁止：**prohibit** doing 禁止做；be **prohibited** from 被禁止
- **monitor** v./n. 监测：**monitor** the situation 监测情况；**monitor** the data 监测数据
- **sympathy** n. 同情：have **sympathy** for 同情；**sympathetic** adj. 同情的
- **numerous** adj. 众多的：**numerous** studies 大量研究；**numerous** times 多次
- **destruction** n. 破坏：the **destruction** of 对……的破坏；**destroy** v. 破坏
- **shortage** n. 短缺：a **shortage** of  ……的短缺；**short** adj. 短缺的
- **extract** v. 提取：**extract** oil 提取油；**extraction** n. 提取
- **legal** adj. 合法的：**legal** rights 合法权利；**illegal** adj. 非法的
- **diversity** n. 多样性：biological **diversity** 生物多样性；**diverse** adj. 多样的

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be prohibited from | 被禁止 | Plastic bags **are prohibited from** being used. |
| a shortage of | ……短缺 | There is **a shortage of** clean water. |
| be monitored by | 被……监测 | The air **is monitored by** sensors. |
| have sympathy for | 同情 | We **have sympathy for** the farmers. |
| be known to | 为……所知 | The practice **is known to** cause harm. |
| in terms of | 在……方面 | **In terms of** protein, beans are rich. |
| contribute to | 有助于；导致 | Farming **contributes to** pollution. |
| be exposed to | 接触到 | Animals **are exposed to** chemicals. |

### 📐 语法：被动语态的各种形式

| 形式 | 结构 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般现在 | am/is/are done | Food **is wasted** every day. |
| 一般过去 | was/were done | The forest **was destroyed**. |
| 一般将来 | will be done | New rules **will be made**. |
| 现在完成 | has/have been done | The law **has been passed**. |
| 情态动词 | can/must be done | Waste **must be reduced**. |
| 不定式 | to be done | The report **is said to be** true. |
| 现在分词 | being done | **Being asked** to comment, she agreed. |
| It is said that | it + be + done + that | **It is said that** the diet is healthy. |

**要点**

1. 被动语态的时态体现在 be 上
2. it is said/reported/believed that... 是常见被动句型
3. 不定式/分词的被动形式：to be done / being done / having been done

**易错边界**

1. 漏 be：The law passed last year.（若强调"被通过"，用 was passed）
2. 不定式被动遗漏：The report is said **to be** true（不是 to true）
3. 通篇被动导致主语缺失：读者不知道动作执行者

**三个练习**

1. The forest ________ (destroy) by fire last year. → **was destroyed**
2. It ________ (say) that the diet is good for health. → **is said**
3. 改错：The plan is said to carried out next month. → **to be carried out**

### ✍️ 写作：正反观点文章（素食）

**结构**：引出话题 → 支持素食的 2 个理由 → 反对的 2 个理由 → 自己的观点

**五个可套用句式**

1. **Whether to... has become a hot topic.** 是否……已成为热门话题。
2. **Supporters believe that...** 支持者认为……
3. **On the other hand, some argue that...** 另一方面，有人认为……
4. **From a nutritional point of view, ...** 从营养角度看，……
5. **A balanced diet seems to be the best choice.** 均衡饮食似乎是最好的选择。

### 📖 阅读技能：识别论证结构

议论文常见"主张—论据—反驳—结论"。注意 **however, on the other hand, although** 后的让步与转折，那往往是作者立场的落点。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：species / prohibit / monitor / shortage / diversity

1. Many ______ are threatened by habitat loss.
2. The government will ______ the use of plastic bags.
3. Scientists ______ the water quality every week.
4. There is a ______ of fresh vegetables in winter.
5. Protecting ______ is essential for the planet.

**解析**：1. species　2. prohibit　3. monitor　4. shortage　5. diversity

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题 species 单复数同形。

**变式**：用 be prohibited from 造句，表达"这里禁止吸烟"。→ Smoking is prohibited here.（或 People are prohibited from smoking here.）

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

把下列句子改为被动语态：

1. People say that the diet is healthy.
2. They must reduce food waste.
3. We are monitoring the situation.

**解析**：

1. **It is said that** the diet is healthy.（或 The diet **is said to be** healthy.）
2. Food waste **must be reduced**.
3. The situation **is being monitored**.

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 题两种被动句型任选其一，但不能写成 People are said that...

**变式**：把 "They reported that the river had been polluted." 改为 "It..." 句型。→ It was reported that the river had been polluted.

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 据说，畜牧业对环境的影响比许多人想象的大。
2. 为了减少浪费，塑料袋在这里被禁止使用。
3. 从营养角度看，均衡饮食比完全素食更现实。

**参考译文**：

1. **It is said that** the impact of farming on the environment is greater than many people imagine.
2. To **reduce** waste, plastic bags **are prohibited** here.
3. **From a nutritional point of view**, a balanced diet is more **realistic** than a completely vegetarian one.

**采分点**：① It is said that；② be prohibited + to reduce；③ from a... point of view。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句用 People say that（可，但题目要求"据说"的被动句型）；第 2 句 prohibit 后接 to use（×）。

**变式**：把第 2 句改为主动语态：The government prohibits plastic bags here to reduce waste.

### 30 秒概述

本单元讨论"下一件大事"（The next big thing）：读一篇关于未来科技的想象文，学**同位语从句**，写一份**学校 ICT 使用报告**。词汇集中在科技、研究、数据与评估。

### 30 秒自评

1. 同位语从句常跟在 fact, news, idea, belief 等抽象名词后吗？
2. 同位语从句中的 that 作成分吗？
3. "The news that he told me" 中 that 从句是同位语吗？

> **答案**：1. 是　2. 不作成分（只起连接作用）　3. 不是，是定语从句（that 作 told 的宾语）

### 🔑 核心词汇（10）

- **consultant** n. 顾问：a technology **consultant** 技术顾问；**consult** v. 咨询
- **outline** n./v. 提纲；概述：an **outline** of  ……的提纲；**outline** the plan 概述计划
- **virtual** adj. 虚拟的：**virtual** reality 虚拟现实；**virtually** adv. 几乎
- **underlie** v. 构成……的基础：the idea that **underlies** 构成……基础的想法；**underlying** adj. 潜在的
- **infer** v. 推断：**infer** from 从……推断；**inference** n. 推断
- **domain** n. 领域：the **domain** of  ……的领域；public **domain** 公共领域
- **concrete** adj. 具体的：**concrete** evidence 具体证据；**concrete** examples 具体例子
- **expertise** n. 专业知识：**expertise** in 在……方面的专长；**expert** n. 专家
- **assess** v. 评估：**assess** the risk 评估风险；**assessment** n. 评估
- **demonstrate** v. 证明；演示：**demonstrate** that 证明；**demonstration** n. 演示

### 🧩 短语与搭配（8）

| 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be based on | 基于 | The report **is based on** **concrete** data. |
| have expertise in | 在……有专长 | She **has expertise in** AI. |
| assess the impact | 评估影响 | We need to **assess the impact** of ICT. |
| demonstrate that | 证明 | The study **demonstrates that** technology helps learning. |
| infer from | 从……推断 | We can **infer from** the data that... |

### 📐 语法：同位语从句

| 名词 + that 从句 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| fact | The **fact that** he lied surprised us. |
| news | The **news that** we won spread quickly. |
| idea | The **idea that** robots will replace us worries some. |
| belief | Her **belief that** hard work pays off is strong. |
| evidence | There is **evidence that** ICT improves learning. |

**要点**

1. 同位语从句解释前面抽象名词的**内容**；that 不作成分，不能省略（正式）
2. 定语从句修饰名词，that 作成分（主语/宾语），可省略（作宾语时）
3. 常见抽象名词：fact, news, idea, belief, evidence, hope, doubt, possibility

**易错边界**

1. 同位语从句与定语从句混淆：The news that he told me（定语，that 作宾语）／The news that he won（同位语）
2. 同位语从句用 which：the fact which he lied（×）
3. 从句缺成分却用 that：the idea that he came up with（定语从句，that 作宾语）

**三个练习**

1. The fact ________ she passed the exam is true. → **that**
2. The idea ________ he suggested was creative. → **that/which**（定语从句）
3. 改错：There is evidence which ICT improves learning. → **that**

### ✍️ 写作：学校 ICT 使用报告

**结构**：调查目的与方法 → 主要发现（数据）→ 问题 → 建议

**五个可套用句式**

1. **The report aims to assess...** 本报告旨在评估……
2. **According to the survey, ...% of students...** 根据调查，……%的学生……
3. **It was found that...** 研究发现……
4. **There is evidence that...** 有证据表明……
5. **We recommend that...** 我们建议……

### 📖 阅读技能：区分事实、推断与观点

事实可直接核实；推断由证据推出（**suggest, infer, indicate**）；观点含评价（**should, better, worrying**）。题目问 "What can be inferred?" 时，选"由文中证据支持但未直接说"的选项。

### 例题 1 · L1 词汇

用方框中的词填空：consultant / virtual / infer / concrete / assess

1. We need a technology ______ to help us.
2. The team will ______ the impact of the new app.
3. Can you give a ______ example?
4. From the data, we can ______ that usage is rising.
5. ______ reality is used in some classrooms.

**解析**：1. consultant　2. assess　3. concrete　4. infer　5. Virtual

**💥 陷阱**：第 4 题 infer 后接 from，不能直接接宾语。

**变式**：用 demonstrate that 造句，表达"研究表明科技能提高学习效率"。→ The study demonstrates that technology can improve learning efficiency.

### 例题 2 · L2 语法

用 that 合并为同位语从句：

1. The fact is true. He passed the exam.
2. The news spread quickly. Our team won.
3. There is evidence. ICT improves learning.

**解析**：

1. The fact **that he passed the exam** is true.
2. The news **that our team won** spread quickly.
3. There is evidence **that ICT improves learning**.

**💥 陷阱**：第 3 题用 which 引导（×，同位语从句用 that）。

**变式**：判断并说明："The idea that he came up with is good." 是什么从句？→ 定语从句（that 作 came up with 的宾语）。

### 例题 3 · L3 翻译

翻译下列句子：

1. 有证据表明，合理使用信息技术能提高学习效率。
2. 我们采访了一位技术顾问，他帮助我们评估了这些应用的影响。
3. 事实是，许多学生缺乏数字安全意识，学校应当采取行动。

**参考译文**：

1. There is **evidence that** the proper use of ICT can improve learning efficiency.
2. We interviewed a technology **consultant**, who helped us **assess** the **impact** of these apps.
3. The **fact** is **that** many students **lack** awareness of digital safety, and schools should **take action**.

**采分点**：① evidence that 同位语从句；② who 非限制性定语从句；③ the fact is that 表语从句。

**💥 陷阱**：第 1 句用 which 引导同位语（×）；第 3 句 the fact is because（×）。

**变式**：把第 1 句改为 "It is demonstrated that..." 句型：It is demonstrated that the proper use of ICT can improve learning efficiency.
